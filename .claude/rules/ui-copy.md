# Screen copy

- Plain words. The person on screen is a business user, not an engineer: no table names, ids, JSON, stack traces or role codes.
- Agents are named with their role: North, the industry expert; Lens, the business analyst; Nira, the guide.
- Every suggestion from an agent or a pack is labelled as a suggestion.
- Errors say what happened and what to do next, in one sentence.
- A missing figure reads "Not available yet" or names what is missing; it is never shown as zero.
- Illustrative content carries a visible "Illustrative" tag.
