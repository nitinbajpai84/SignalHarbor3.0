# UC5 synthetic source data: answer key (Are we inside our limits?)

Evergreen Office, month-ends July to October 2026. Generated by `tools/make_uc5_data.py` from `docs/test/10uc_universe.json`; October is built there too. Synthetic only.

## Basis (read before the figures)

- Limit base: the portfolio's investment holdings in USD, cash excluded (the universe's convention). Funds use the latest known manager statement value; securities use the administrator's month-end value.
- Stale: an input older than 90 days at the month end, or not yet verified. A breach with a stale input is cause `stale data`; otherwise `real breach`.
- Early warning: inside the maximum but within 0.25 points of it. This is an assumption of the generator, not a signed rule.
- Limit versions: v1 (in force Jul and Aug) keeps the single-issuer maximum at 6.00%; v2 (from 1 Sep) is 5.00% for Harborview Semiconductor. Manager (55%), listed equity (60 to 90%) and hedge funds (20 to 60%) are the same in both.
- The universe's rules sheet shows the 5.00% single-issuer limit from 1 Jan 2026. The v1 6.00% keeps Harborview inside in July and August on the seeded holdings (5.73%, 5.71%). The conflict is deliberate; see issue 1.
- October security prices are invented (within about 3% of September). October trades follow the universe's trade list and the administrator quantities.

## Planted issues

1. Limit version: the universe rules say 5.00% from 1 Jan 2026; this data uses 6.00% until 31 Aug. Decide which is signed before the run, or Jul and Aug look different.
2. July and August are not 'all inside' on the seeded holdings: Cedarpoint (C plus D over EG-B-OPS) is 57.38% against 55%, a stale breach because Fund D's 31 Mar value is 122 and 153 days old. The honest result is a stale breach each month, not a clean July.
3. No early-warning line appears in August: Harborview is 5.73% (July) and 5.71% (August) against 6.00%, headroom 0.27 and 0.29 points, outside the 0.25 band. The story's 'Aug one warning' is not produced; change the band or the holdings if you want it.
4. September, Harborview Semiconductor is a real breach: 2,368,000.00 of 41,200,000.00 = 5.75% against 5.00%, over by 308,000.00. Inputs recent, cause real breach.
5. September, Cedarpoint is a stale breach: 12,925,000.00 of 22,875,000.00 = 56.50% against 55%. Fund C is 92 days old, Fund D 183 days, Fund E unverified. Flag stale, not a real breach.
6. October, Harborview is resolved: 15,000 shares after the 6 Oct sale of 5,000 (T-20261006-005) booked by the administrator in the 31 Oct file; the 9 Oct UC8 break is closed by then.
7. October, a new real breach from revised valuations: Cedarpoint 14,150,000.00 of 24,100,000.00 = 58.71%, all inputs 30 Sep or later, cause real breach.
8. Pacific Rim Convertible 2029 (about 2.7% of EG-LIQUID) has no asset class in every month: a question, not in any bucket, not a breach.
9. October hedge funds use manager estimates (H1, H2 status 'estimate'). The signed estimate rule allows estimates in the investment report only, so a limit check using them should be flagged.
10. UC8 carry-over in the October file: the administrator holds Northwind Logistics at 60,200 (its 10,200 buy); the trading export says 12,000. The 1,800-share difference is open, value 75,870 at 42.15.
11. The July and August administrator NAV for Cedarpoint's Fund C (8,420,000 in July) differs from the latest known statement (8,275,000, amended). The limit uses the latest known value; the reader should explain the difference, not hide it.
