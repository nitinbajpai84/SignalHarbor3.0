Rules the product should apply (same as the universe):
- Calls "due within 30 days" are those with due date on or before as-of + 30 days (inclusive). 30-day bucket = as-of to as-of + 30. 60-day bucket = to as-of + 60.
- "Confirmed" calls count in the confirmed view. "Noticed" calls (not confirmed) count only in the all-noticed view.
- Cash for liquidity is the administrator closing balance on the last cash day (8 Oct for October).
- Called to date is the office register: confirmed notices only. A notice that is noticed but not confirmed is an outstanding notice, not called.
- Manager call-plan figures in the commitments file are estimates. They are never due and never counted in any bucket.

## 5. Deviations from the brief and the universe (decisions to confirm)

1. October run date. The brief asks for month-end files (31 Oct). The 30-day story only works before the 20 Oct payment, so the October cycle is as at 9 Oct, the same as the universe. A 31 Oct run would have different buckets (the October calls would be past due and the December call would sit at 30 days). Decision needed: keep 9 Oct, or build a separate 31 Oct cycle.
2. Notices outside the universe. These are new and do not appear in the universe's `cash_movements`: the 15 Sep Saltmarsh notice (550,000.00), the 29 Sep amendment (600,000.00), and the 23 Jul payment and 3 Sep receipt in the backfilled cash. The 6 Jul late notice, the 1 Sep distribution and the four October notices are from the universe.
3. Backfilled cash. The universe starts Entity B cash at 9 Sep. July and August are backfilled, and 1 to 8 Sep are backfilled, to meet the universe anchor of 851,099.16 on 8 Sep. July and August are synthetic and are not in the universe.
4. Fourth source. The brief lists three sources. The administrator cash file (880,400.00 is an administrator figure) and the notice receipt log were added because the gold cannot be reproduced without them.
5. Commitments "called" uses confirmed notices only, as the universe does. The Excel also lists the outstanding notice separately.
6. The Trust bank vs trading export gap (1,800.00 on 8 Oct, universe P08) is not in these sources. It is not part of UC6.

## 6. Judgment for Raj Patel

On 8 Oct the bank balance for Entity B is 868,000.00, not 880,400.00. The administrator figure leaves out the 12,400.00 wire that already left the bank. Measured on bank cash, the 30-day shortfall after both noticed calls is -182,000.00 (868,000.00 - 1,050,000.00), not -169,600.00. The gold uses the administrator figure (the universe's official figure). The product should show the timing item, or say which figure it uses.
