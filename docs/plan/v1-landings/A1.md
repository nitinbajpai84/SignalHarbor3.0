## What landed

- `sh_platform/v1/a1_chat.py`: the engine. Context = the signed intent of work (read-only), design lines, scan per measure, mapping summary with unclear rows, data model, questions, proposed scope and the last twelve turns, flattened and trimmed under 13,000 characters. Measures go to the model as letter aliases (`ref: a`). The model answers `{reply, run, data_model, questions, scope, ready}`. `run` calls b1 `design`, b2 `run_scan` and b3 `draft` in order, and each step adds one factual line to Span's turn (`did`). If the model is off, Span takes the next missing step per message, labelled "Span (rules only)", and its questions come from `skills/span/questions.md`. Approval: unanswered questions take their defaults, the proposed scope (filtered to available or partly) goes through b2 `agree`, the state becomes approved, and a room hand-over goes to Flow.
- `sh_platform/v1/a1_routes.py`:
  - `GET /api/v1/build/{iid}/blueprint/chat`
  - `POST …/blueprint/chat` {text} (architect seat)
  - `POST …/blueprint/approve` (company_admin only)
  - A switched-off Build centre is refused.
- `sh_platform/v1/a1.permissions.yaml`.
- Record `build/blueprint_conversation/<iid>.json` `{intent_id, turns, state: open|ready|approved, approved_by, approved_at, data_model, questions, scope}`.
- `skills/span/` `playbook.md`, `questions.md`, `traps.md`, `examples.md`: a family-office data architect's playbook covering:
  - the three layers, grain, keys and conformed dimensions
  - slowly changing data, lineage and the finance reconciliation ties
  - what to ask the head of data, with defaults
  - traps such as summing balances, averaging ratios, a renamed column, a missing currency and duplicates
  
  The four files total 4.2k characters, under the loader's 6,000 limit.
- `ui/src/v1/a1/` `routes.tsx` (`build/:iid`), `api.ts`, `SpanPage.tsx`, `a1.test.tsx`:
  - Left: the conversation.
  - Right: a Context box (the signed intent of work, measures, the catalogue by layer, what Span has drafted with links to the b1/b2/b3 pages, and the skill's name).
  - Below that: a "For the head of data" summary with **Approve design and mapping for the engineer**, then the tabs **Design** (Archify-style layers), **Data model** (fact/dimension diagram and table), **Questions for the head of data** ("Answer in the conversation" pre-fills the composer) and **Scan**.
  - On a phone the right column stacks above the chat.

## How to test by hand (served on 8400)

1. Sign in as Arjun Mehta (Neera). Sign an intent of work: talk to North at `/centre/intent`, then sign on `/centre/intent/<iid>/sow`.
2. Sign in as Leela Raman (analyst, the architect). Open `/centre/build/<iid>`. Span greets her and names the number of measures and sources.
3. Write "Please start." Span drafts the design, and the reply shows a "Design drafted: …" tag. The Design tab draws Sources → Landing → Cleaned → Report.
4. Write "Go on." Span runs the scan, and the Scan tab shows "These N of M can be built now".
5. Write "And the mapping." Span writes the mapping sheet (link in Context), and the Data model and Questions tabs fill in. "For the head of data" appears, but Leela sees "The head of data approves this."
6. Optional: on the Questions tab press "Answer in the conversation", type an answer and send. With the model on, Span settles the question.
7. Sign in as Arjun (company_admin, the head of data). Open the same page and press **Approve design and mapping for the engineer**. The panel then reads "Approved by Arjun Mehta … handed to Flow". The room `/centre/intent/<iid>/room` has Span's hand-over to Flow, and the composer is closed.

With the model off, every Span turn is labelled "Span (rules only)".
