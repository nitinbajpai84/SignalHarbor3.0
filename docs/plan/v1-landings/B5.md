## What landed
- `sh_platform/v1/b5_ops.py`: `summary(plane, today)`, `mark(plane, body, by)`, `loaded(plane)`. A newly opened alert posts once to the room (`by="watcher"`, `kind="note"`, `to=["flow","people"]`, ref `{"kind":"run"}`) of every intent whose SOW lists the source (`sources[].id` or `name`). A Loaded / Not loaded act posts a note with ref `{"kind":"loaded","id":"<period>:<source>"}`. Both imports of `sh_platform.v1.room.post` are guarded.

- `sh_platform/v1/b5.permissions.yaml`: both routes for company_admin, analyst (the ops manager seat).
- `ui/src/v1/b5/routes.tsx` (`build/ops`), `ui/src/v1/b5/OpsPage.tsx`, `ui/src/v1/b5/OpsPage.test.tsx`.
