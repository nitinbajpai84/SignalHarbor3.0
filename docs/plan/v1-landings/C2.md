## What landed

- `sh_platform/v1/c2_offers.py`: `offers(plane, pack, iid)`, `have(plane, pack, iid, feed_id, by)`. A feed is offered when some field has `required_for` and no SOW source covers it (source id/name/holds names the feed, or its fields carry every required field). Named by kind only ("FX rates feed"). Sorted by measures already in the SOW, then by measures unlocked.
- `sh_platform/v1/c2_routes.py`: `GET /api/v1/consume/offers[?intent=iid]` (newest SOW when no intent given; returns `can_answer`), `POST /api/v1/consume/offers/{id}/have` body `{intent_id?}`: adds source `src_<feed>` (kind system, fields from the pack) and unclear `u_<feed>`, `state: draft`, `signature: null`, `version + 1`; posts a `note` to North in the room with a `sow` ref. 409 when the SOW already has it, 404 unknown offer or intent, 403 for anyone not the decision maker.
- `sh_platform/v1/c2.permissions.yaml`: GET for all six company roles; POST for company_admin, sponsor (checked again in the handler through `seats.require_seat`).
- `ui/src/v1/c2/OffersSection.tsx` (the section), `ui/src/v1/c2/routes.tsx` (`/centre/consume/offers`, a standalone page until C1 sets the section in), `ui/src/v1/c2/OffersSection.test.tsx`.
- `tests/v1/test_c2.py`: offers on Neera minus held sources, "We have it" writes draft v+1 and a room post, 409 on repeat, analyst 403, Fresh shows an empty line.

Tests: `python -m pytest tests/v1/test_c2.py` 3 passed; `npx vitest run src/v1/c2` 2 passed; `tsc --noEmit` clean for c2.

## Open questions

- The contract names no source owner or sample path for an offered feed: both are written as `null`, and the unclear line asks who sends it, from which system, from which month (`who_can_settle: "Decision maker"`, a free-text label since the contract gives no shape).
- Sending a signed SOW back to draft clears `signature`; the contract says "back to draft version +1" but not what happens to the old signature or whether the glossary/catalogue rows written at signing are marked superseded. I4 should decide.
- Neera's KPI codes from North may not be the pack's K-codes, so `in_sow` is true only when the SOW reuses a pack code (pack-suggested KPIs).
- The pack's feed list is the internal workbook feeds (FX rates is the one true market feed); there is no "market" tag in `feeds.yaml`, so every uncovered feed that unlocks a measure is offered.
