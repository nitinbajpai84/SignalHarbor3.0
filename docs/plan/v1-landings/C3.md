## What landed
- `sh_platform/v1/c3_chat.py`: Lumen. Eight goals (existing dashboards, KPI priority and order, comparisons, outside feeds, what-if levers, drivers, audiences, refresh). It writes the brief `consume/brief/<iid>.json` {views, feeds_wanted, levers, existing_dashboards, end_user_inputs, notes}. The conversation goes to `consume/chat/<iid>.json` in the thread's shape. The model sees KPI names and feed kinds only. Codes (`K01`) and feed ids (`fx_rates`) are mapped back after the reply, and a name that is not signed is dropped. Dropped dashboards are saved under `consume/brief/files/<iid>/`; only a preview of up to 2,000 characters goes to the model. With the model off, "Lumen (rules only)" asks one fixed question per goal. The end users' short version has three fixed questions; each answer goes into `end_user_inputs` {by, question, answer, at}. There are also helpers for C1 and C2: `kpi_order(plane, iid)` and `feeds_wanted(plane, iid)`.
- `sh_platform/v1/c3_routes.py` and `c3.permissions.yaml`:
  - `GET /api/v1/consume/brief?iid=`: any seat. Without an iid it opens the intent whose statement of work changed last.
  - `POST /api/v1/consume/brief/{iid}/chat` {text, files?}: analyst, company_admin or sponsor.
  - `POST /api/v1/consume/brief/{iid}/input` {text}: any seat.
  - The routes sit under `/consume/brief/...` so they never meet C1's `/api/v1/consume/{iid}/{code}`.
- `skills/consumer/`: `playbook.md`, `questions.md`, `traps.md`, `examples.md` (how an analyst interviews readers).
- `ui/src/v1/c3/`:

## How to test by hand (8400)
1. Sign in as Leela Raman (analyst). Open `/centre/consume/design` (or, once wired, press "Shape what you see" on the Consume page). Lumen greets you and asks for an existing dashboard.
2. Press the paper-clip and attach a CSV or text file describing a pack, then send "This is our quarterly pack". The Context box shows "1 brought in", and the Brief tab lists it.
3. Answer: "Time-weighted return first, then cash drag", "against the benchmark and last quarter", "bring in FX rates", "what if we withdraw more cash", "idle cash pulls the return down", "the committee", "monthly". The Brief fills in. Preview shows a box per figure in that order, the lever as a slider and the feed by kind. After the last goal, Lumen reads the brief back.
4. Copy the "open the short questions" link (`/centre/consume/design/<iid>/ask`). Sign in as Dev Sharma (participant) and answer the three questions. Back as Leela, "What readers said" lists them.
5. As Dev, sending on `/centre/consume/design` shows the plain refusal.
6. With the model off, turns are named "Lumen (rules only)". Fresh (no statement of work) shows "Nothing to shape yet".
