### G3
- Record `intents/<iid>/benchmarks.json`: rows `{id, code, kpi, dims{period, entity, ...}, reported, expected, tolerance, source, source_doc, line, stated_by, proposed, dropped, status}`. Status is `ready`, `needs figure` (blocks signing) or `proposed` (Span's, only for a KPI the owner gave no rows for).
- From words: one row per line holding a figure, the label becomes the dimension value, dimension names from `by entity by month`. A dimension named with no figure creates a `needs figure` row and North asks again (the benchmarks goal is not counted covered).
- From a report: markdown/table/xlsx tables (KPI as a column, or KPI as a row with periods across) and plain `label: value` lines; the row keeps the file and line number it came from.
- Signing: the raiser, plus the owner of any KPI on the sheet who is a known person and not the raiser. Any change to a signed sheet reopens it (version + 1, signatures history kept). Artefact `benchmarks` in the project (readable, commentable, `export?format=xlsx|md|docx`).
- Flow after the load: `verify` compares each row at its granularity (gold: first column period, last column figure, middle columns matched by name to the dimensions); no matching column is said ("the gold table has no entity column"), never guessed. Each failing row is a comment (build pack) to the raiser and the sponsor.

### G4
- `classify`: a change of source field/table or formula/SQL, or dropping a row, is a deviation; anything else is Flow's own fix ("runs the load again without asking", 409).
- A deviation is a comment of kind `approval` to the head of data (and the raiser when `business_definition`), with before, after, why and the rows it fixes. Only "approved" or "not approved" is accepted as the answer. All addressees approve: the mapping row or sheet row changes, a signed amendment `A<n>` is added to the design record and a note goes on the design (the trail). Any "not approved" closes it, closes the other ask, and the reason goes to Flow (`declined_deviations`). The same change is not asked twice.
- A miss with no loaded figure is always raised: a plain comment to the head of data (`raise_unresolved`), once.
- `g4_deviations.pending_block` and `P4.sign_block` are what G2's go-live can call ("every miss resolved").

## How to test by hand (sign-ins in `data/_control/seed_credentials.txt`; company neera)
1. As Arjun (decision maker), open North and answer the benchmarks question with lines such as "Net worth by entity for September 2026:" then "Tan Family Trust 12,400,000" and "Lim Holdings 8,100,000". `GET /api/v1/build/<iid>/benchmarks/sheet` shows the rows; the KPI's old `benchmark` is a pointer. Say "Cash drag by account by month" with no figures: a `needs figure` row appears and North asks again.
2. Signing: `POST .../benchmarks/sheet/sign` is refused with the sentence naming the gaps; type a figure with `POST .../sheet/rows/<rid>` `{"reported": "950"}`, then sign as Arjun (Leela is refused). Or put a report in the company's `documents/` and `POST .../sheet/read {"file": "report.md"}`: rows appear with "report.md, line N".
3. Spreadsheet: `GET /api/v1/projects/<pid>/artefacts/benchmarks/export?format=xlsx`.
4. Load as Leela (`POST /api/v1/build/<iid>/load`): a failing row becomes a comment on the build pack to Arjun and Priya.
5. As Leela, `POST /api/v1/build/<iid>/deviations` with `{summary, about, before, after, why, effect:[ref], change:{source_field:"..."}, mapping_row:1}`; Leela's Pulse shows the approval card; answer `POST .../deviations/<did>/decide {"decision":"approved"}` (or "not approved" with `reason`). The mapping row changes only then; the design view shows the amendment.
6. Automated: `SH_MODEL_PROVIDER=off python -m pytest tests/v1/test_g3g4.py tests/v1/test_p4.py`.

## Open questions
- Who owns a KPI is a free-text `owner` on the SOW; only an owner who matches a known person (project people or someone who spoke in the intent thread) is asked to sign.
- Flow does not decide on its own that a miss needs a deviation: the engineer's seat (or Flow's later turn) raises it through the route; there is no automatic proposer yet.
- An approved change is made at once but only proven by the next load; after approval the miss counts as resolved for signing and go-live.
- KPI business-definition changes are recorded in the amendment but not written to the signed intent of work (it is frozen).

## Fixes (review round 9)
- **Approval proves nothing alone (B2).** `covered()` is gone: a miss clears only when the next load writes `met: true`, or its comment was answered by the addressee and resolved. An approved change that could not be made stays `status: approved` with `implement_error`; `pending_block` says "approved, not yet made". This replaces the old open question ("after approval the miss counts as resolved"). Tests: `test_deviation_needs_approval...`, `test_failed_implementation_stays_approved_not_made`.
- **Answered is not resolved (S3/S4).** `p4_benchmarks.sign_block` counts `resolved` with an answer only (so the build pack, and go-live through it, wait for the signer to resolve). Tests: `test_p4` (sign refused after the answer, passes after the resolve), `test_g2` (go live refused by an unresolved miss, S8).
- **The raiser never approves (B3).** `post_deviation` records `by=viewer.name`; `G4.check_answerer` is called by `post_decide` and by `P1.answer` for deviation approvals (403 "You raised this request; another approver answers it."). No head of data named: `propose` refuses 409 instead of falling to the raiser. Tests: the deviation test (Leela raises, is refused, Priya approves), `test_no_head_of_data_is_refused...`.
- **Only "approved" / "not approved" answers (S6).** Bare "ok" and "yes" are refused. The Approve and Not this buttons already send those words.
- **Locked documents (B4).** `sheet/read` takes `{file}` or `{item_id}`, looks the item up in the library and refuses 403 unless `readable(item, viewer)` (K1); a file with no library row is refused 404. The row's `source` is now "file, line N" with no line text. Tests: `test_a_locked_document_is_not_read_into_the_sheet`, `test_source_keeps_no_line_text...`.
- **Gate and sheet (S1, S2, S5).** `p1_project.document` gates `benchmarks` (read and export) to company_admin/analyst/sponsor; a sheet with nobody named to sign cannot be signed ("Nobody is named to sign this sheet yet; sign the intent of work first."); a line with two figures ("A 1 and B 2", ";" or ",") becomes two rows, a line that says "expected / within / tolerance" stays one. Tests: `test_participant_does_not_read_the_benchmark_artefact`, `test_a_sheet_with_nobody_to_sign_and_two_figures_on_a_line`.
- **UI gaps closed.** "Drop last month's report" on the sheet (uploads through `/api/v1/library`, then `sheet/read {item_id}`); the source link opens `/centre/build/:iid/source?file=&line=` (`SourceDocPage`, served by `GET .../sheet/doc`, K1-checked, line marked); the Pulse approval card links to `/centre/build/:iid/deviations`; `deviation` is in f1's `PointKind` (no cast).
