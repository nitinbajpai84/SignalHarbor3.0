## How it works
- Definitions come from every intent of work that has been signed, on each read (nothing is copied); only what a person decided is stored, in `graph/k2.json` per company (concepts, assignment, merge proposals, choices, accepted edits, suggestions, difference lines).
- Concept = canonical KPI. Same normalised name goes under the same concept (that is what makes variants). Pack synonym groups (glossary / kpi_tree entries with `synonyms` or `aliases`, or a `synonyms` section with `groups`, read through the pack loader for the company's industry) and word overlap make a **merge proposal**; for word overlap the fast model is asked through the router when there is one ("same measure?"; "no" drops the proposal and is remembered). Nothing merges until an owner of either side, or a company admin, confirms; a decline is remembered.
- Difference line: written by the fast model from the definitions only (digit-free prompt, through the gateway), regenerated when any definition of the concept changes; with `SH_MODEL_PROVIDER=off` a program-built line stands in (`by: stub`).
- Suggest a change: `POST /api/v1/map/suggestions` (change definition, merge, rename, add synonym, change owner, other) creates a P1 comment of kind `suggestion` on the intent of work, addressed to the definition's owner (a concept's owner is the owner of its oldest definition). Accept: a new version of the definition (overlay with who, when, who suggested), signed text untouched. Decline needs a reason. It shows in Pulse for the owner.
- Routes: `GET /api/v1/map/concepts[/{id}]`, `GET|POST /api/v1/map/proposals[/{pid}]`, `GET /api/v1/map/collision?kpi=&iid=`, `POST /api/v1/map/choice`, `GET|POST /api/v1/map/suggestions`, `POST .../{cid}/accept|decline`, `GET /api/v1/map/ask?q=`.
- Access: a definition of a project the viewer may not see (`may_see_project == "none"`, i.e. Restricted and not on the team) is absent from concepts, ask, collision, proposals, Pulse and suggestions, and its map node is the team's (G1 `restrict_nodes`).

## Wiring North (done in `i6_chat`; K2 did not touch it)
Python API: `from sh_platform.v1 import k2_map` then
- `k2_map.collision_fact(plane, viewer, kpi_name, exclude_iid=iid) -> dict | None`: `{"concept": {"id","name"}, "definitions": [{"id","team","owner","project","project_id","definition",...}], "question": "<name> already has two definitions in the company: Finance and Performance. Do you mean one of these, or is yours different?", "choices": ["concept","variant_of","new"]}`. Every id and name is filled by the program; the count is spelled out so the reply guard has no digit to rewrite.
- `k2_map.record_choice(plane, iid, kpi, choice)`; `choice` is `"new"` or `{"choice": "concept", "concept": <concept id>}` (it is one of the existing definitions), `{"choice": "variant_of", "variant_of": <definition id>, "reason": "..."}` (different; a reason is required), `{"choice": "new"}`. Stored in `graph/k2.json` and written on the KPI in the draft intent of work as `concept`, `variant_of` (+ `variant_reason`) or `new`. `k2_map.choice_for(plane, iid, code)` reads it back for the Definitions document.

The wiring is done by the orchestrator in `i6_chat._collision` / `i6_chat._record_collision`: `_collision` builds one flat string key (`company_definitions_to_check`, letters A, B... mapped back by the program) before `fit()`, registers the owners' and teams' names with the vault, and leaves `ctx["facts"]` alone; `_record_collision` calls `record_choice` with a program-checked choice. The snippet this note first proposed is withdrawn (it put a string in the list of figure rows and a list of definitions where the gateway refuses lists).

## How to test on the demo (3 to 5 steps)
1. Sign two intents of work that name "Net worth" (or use Ardmore's). Open COMPANY MAP, search "what does net worth mean?": the concept appears with both variants (team, project, text, owner) and the one-line difference.
2. Sign a third intent with "Net worth after fees": the map shows "Possibly the same measure" with Yes / Not the same; nothing is merged until the owner presses Yes.
3. Open a Definition node, press "Suggest a change", propose new text. Sign in as the owner: Pulse shows "1 suggestion waits for your answer"; accept (or decline with a reason). The node now says version 2.
4. As the owner of the first definition, open Pulse after a second variant is signed: "Your Net worth definition now has a second variant: Performance's."
5. As someone off a Restricted project's team, repeat step 1: its definition is not listed.

## Known limits
- Signed intents of work stay read-only: an accepted change is an overlay version in `graph/k2.json`, not a rewrite of `sow.json`. The Definitions document and the exports should read `k2_map.concepts` / the definition's `version` if they must show it.
- `team` is the KPI's `team` field, else its owner's name; the pack has no department field for a person yet.
- The pack has no KPI synonym section today: groups are read from `synonyms`/`aliases` on glossary and kpi_tree entries or an optional `synonyms.yaml` (`groups: [[a, b]]`); until a pack adds them only name equality and word overlap propose.
- Word-overlap rule: Jaccard 0.5 or one name's words inside the other's (at least two words). It will propose some pairs a person declines; a decline is remembered.
- The "Pulse variant" line shows for 14 days after the new variant is signed.
- The model is asked only by the background job (see Fixes after review); a failed call leaves the rules' proposal or the stub line.

## Fixes after review

- B1 names to the vault: `_ask_json(..., names=)` calls `register_names(plane, "", names)` before the router and `run_agent`; `_same` and `_diff_line` pass the owners, teams and project titles of the definitions involved, so the labels and any name typed in a definition go out tokenised, as in `i6_chat.ask`.
- B2 wiring: this note now says the wiring is done in `i6_chat._collision` / `_record_collision`.
- Difference line after an accepted edit: a changed concept gets the program's line at once (`by: stub`) and the stored key records which text the model has seen (`asked`), so the background job wording replaces the stub after every accepted change instead of the stub staying for good.
- No model call in a rebuild or a read: `_sync` (and so `add_nodes`, every GET, `suggest`, `decide_proposal`, `record_choice`, `answer_suggestion`) is rules only. `enrich(plane, model, cap=5)` is the one place the fast model is asked: the stub lines and the word-overlap proposals (marked `check`; the model's "different" drops the proposal and is remembered). It runs in a thread started by `_kick` after a write or a read finds work (at most once in 30 s per company, none with the model off), calls outside the store lock, and applies an answer only if the text it was about is unchanged. `sync(plane, model)` with a model runs the job on the spot (tests).
- Internal projects: G1's artefact rule. `_See` asks `may_see_project` once per project per call: "none" hides the definition everywhere (as before); "preview" (Internal, not on the team) shows the concept name, the team and the project title only (no text, formula, owner, unit, period or why-different; `hidden: true`), and the concept's difference line is built only from the variants whose text the viewer may read. On the map, `m1_routes._map` calls `k2_map.gate_nodes`, which blanks the text, owner and difference line of such Definition and Concept nodes (nodes now carry `project_id` / `project_ids`).
- Suggest on a concept whose oldest definition is hidden: the target is the oldest definition the viewer can see.
- A team-name owner: `_answerer` addresses the suggestion to the owner when they are a person with an account, else the project's owner, else a company admin (no directory: the owner as written).
- Cost: projects are read once per sync (`_projects`), G1 once per project per call, the pack's synonyms only when a new name needs them, `proposals` is shared with `pulse_items` and `suggest` syncs once.
- Reopened intents: the last signed kpis are kept in `graph/k2.json` (`signed`) each time the map sees the record signed; a reopened draft shows those, not the draft. A record reopened before the map ever saw it signed has no signed text and is left out until it is signed again.
- `POST /api/v1/map/choice`: the intent must exist (`P.project_of`) and the caller must be on its project (`G1.require(..., "full")`); `record_choice` itself refuses an empty, malformed or unknown intent (404), an empty KPI (400) and caps the KPI at 200 characters.
- Nits taken: `answer_suggestion` applies the change before it answers the comment; `_apply` follows a merged-away concept with `_live`.
- Not changed: the "Worth a look" line for the other side after a merge, `K2Panel` fetching proposals per page, the comments on write routes in the permissions yaml (nits, left).
