- `tests/v1/test_m1.py`.

What the map draws per 1.0 intent (every record optional; a damaged intent is skipped, never the whole map):
- Intent (goal, why, readers, needed by, frequency from the KPI periods, state talking / signed / designed / loaded / live).
- Person and Team with their role per intent (decision maker = first person in the thread; spoke to North, architect, data engineer, consumer from the thread, room, A1, F1 and C3 conversations; owner of a KPI or source; approver from an approval rule; reader from goal.readers). A name we know or a full name is a person; anything else ("Operations", "The investment committee") a team.
- Process and Steps (who does it, the system or source used, `manual` when no system).
- Source, Document (I5's source of kind document is retyped to Document: one node), what was read (definitions, owners, rules with file and quote), Fields (first 60 per source), Tables (layer, meaning), loaded periods per source.
- KPI as I5's `measure:<code>`, or `measure:<iid>:<code>` when the same code means different KPIs in two intents (open question 1), definition, formula, unit, owner; Term from the company glossary; a dashed "No source yet" stub (type Gap) with the scan's missing items or "the intent of work names no source for it yet".
- Rule (governance and rules read from documents) with governed_by to what it applies to; Decision for each settled unclear point.
- Signed: every intent of work version (version n supersedes n-1, signed_by the signer), the approved design (A1), the mapping sheet (B3, tables derived_from sources), the consumption brief (C3) and its Views (KPIs shown_in, audience asked_for).

On the demo companies (built on a copy): Ardmore about 330 nodes and 730 edges over 4 intents; Neera about 190 nodes and 330 edges; Fresh 0.

## How to test by hand (after the shared edits below, on 8400)
1. Sign in as Arjun Mehta (Neera, decision maker). Click COMPANY MAP. The intent "I have to run the monthly performance report…" shows as one cluster with its state; "The rest of the company" holds people and systems from the profile.
2. Click the intent: its things appear by kind (people and teams, how it is done today, sources and documents, KPIs and terms, tables and fields, rules and decisions, signed documents and views). The dashed chip "No source yet" is a KPI with nothing feeding it; click it to read why.
3. Click a KPI: the right card says what it is, who said it and when, where it came from (in words, never a path), what it connects to and what depends on it; "Open the record" goes to the intent.
4. Search "Where does net worth come from?" (on Ardmore) or "time-weighted return" (on Neera): matching things list; click one.
5. Sign out; sign in as Dev Sharma (participant). The same map, read-only. Open a KPI, click "Something looks wrong", write a line, Send. The node now says "1 note from Dev Sharma, 8 Oct".
6. As Arjun: `GET /api/v1/map/comments` lists Dev's note (for Home).

## Open questions
1. I5 keys a KPI as `measure:<code>`, but codes are per intent (Ardmore has four K01s with different names), so I5 merges different KPIs into one node. M1 finds codes whose names differ across intents, drops I5's merged node (only when every provenance is a 1.0 intent of work) and redraws those as `measure:<iid>:<code>`; a code used once keeps I5's id, so I5's test still passes. Better long term: I5 uses the per-intent id itself; then `_drop_shared_measures` in m1_map.py can go.
2. Tables are keyed by name (I5's `table_id`); Ardmore's gold tables are named after KPI codes (k01), so two intents' k01 tables merge. Left as is; the node lists both intents.
3. The contract has no "settled by" or "settled at" on an unclear point: a Decision shows `who_can_settle` and no date.
4. `graph/comments.json` keeps the 2.0 shape (keyed by node id) with `state: open|seen` added; a 2.0 NodePage reading a "seen" note shows it as decided.
5. Runs (warehouse/runs.json) are not drawn as nodes; a source shows its Loaded periods only.
