Contract for P2, P4 and P5:
- `comment(plane, pid_or_iid, artefact, line_ref, by, to, text, *, kind="comment")`
  - `artefact`: `intent`, `design`, `build_pack` or `brief`.
  - `to`: a person's name, or a part on the project (`raiser`, `sponsor`, `builder`, `head_of_data`, `ops`). A part becomes the name that holds it, or the raiser's name when nobody holds it yet.
  - `kind`: `comment` (the default), `handover`, `note` or `ticket`. Only `handover` and `note` never hold a signature.
  - Returns None when the intent has no project.
- A comment record is `{id, artefact, line_ref, by, name, to, text, kind, state: open|answered|resolved, at, answer, answered_at}`. A resolved comment also carries `resolved_by` and `resolved_at`.
- State and signature for the intent and design artefacts are read live from `sow.json` and `blueprint_conversation/<iid>.json`. The build pack and brief are marked with `signed(...)`.
- Stage moves talking → signed → designed from the records. Built and live come from `set_stage`.
- Pulse deep link: `/centre/project/<pid>?artefact=<kind>&line=<ref>&comment=<cid>` opens that artefact, scrolls to the line and outlines the comment (`aria-current="true"`).

Routes:
- `GET /api/v1/projects`

- `GET /api/v1/projects/{pid}`
- `GET /api/v1/projects/{pid}/artefacts/{kind}` (adds `me`)

- `GET` and `POST /api/v1/projects/{pid}/comments`
- `POST …/comments/{cid}/answer`
- `POST …/comments/{cid}/resolve`
