Bands:
- **Needs you**: X2's waits, plus one card per artefact for open comments addressed to the person ("2 comments on the system design need your answer", opens `/centre/project/<pid>?artefact=<kind>&line=<line_ref>&comment=<id>` at the first open one), tickets (`kind: ticket`) and hand-overs (`kind: handover`, "Accept the hand-over") the same way, and "Flow asked you to confirm N formulas" (data engineer seat, opens `/centre/build/<iid>/engineer`). P1 absent: no `projects/` folder, so Needs you is exactly what X2 showed.
- **Your projects today**: one line per intent (with its P1 project when one exists): stage (project's, else X2's), last load (latest period of its sources), the figure that moved most since the last period, open comments, open tickets.
- **Worth a look**: a loaded figure that jumped over 20 % or broke its range (C1), a source with no file since <day> (ops seat only, as `GET /api/v1/ops`), the review queue (company_admin, sponsor, analyst, as `GET /api/v1/extract/queue`), open map notes on nodes the viewer's map shows, and items under `signals/*.json`.

Visibility: a project not yet signed shows only to the decision maker seat and to people named in the project's `people`; signed ones to every role (as C1). Comments show only to their addressee (matched by account id, or by name unless the name is shared).
