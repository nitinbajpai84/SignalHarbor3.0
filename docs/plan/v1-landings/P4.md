# P4 Benchmarks in the intent of work and the design

**Summary**
1. North asks item 16, **benchmarks**, right after the KPIs: what was reported for the last period, what it should read after the load, within how much. "I don't have one" stores `benchmark: null`.
2. The figures are copied by the program from the person's own words (`p4_benchmarks.parse`). The model only marks a KPI `"benchmark": "given"` or `"none"`, so the reply guard never has to pass a figure.
3. Span's design carries one benchmark per KPI (`benchmarks` in the A1 view, frozen on the record at approval). It is either the SOW's, or a proposed one marked `proposed: true` that the raiser confirms against the first load.
4. After every load (b4) Flow checks each gold figure against its benchmark and writes `build/verify/<iid>.json`. Each miss becomes one P1 comment on the build pack to the raiser and one to the sponsor, raised once and not again on reloads.
5. `POST /api/v1/build/{iid}/pack/sign` signs the build pack through P1 and moves the project to `built`. It is refused (409, plain sentence) while any miss has no answered comment, or while a comment addressed to the signer is open.

## Open questions
- A KPI with no benchmark (proposed) cannot be compared, so the check records `met: null` and still asks the raiser to confirm the loaded figure. This counts as a miss for signing. That is my reading of "every ambiguity is raised to the business user".
- Tolerance when the person names none: 1 % of the expected figure (`DEFAULT_TOLERANCE_PCT`). A proposed benchmark carries `tolerance: null` and says the same 1 % in its `source`. The card names no default value.
- Units are not normalised: "4.2 %" is stored as 4.2. If a gold table holds the fraction (0.042), the check reports a miss and the comment carries both figures.
- A period said without a year means the latest such month before the current one. No period said means the last full month.
- The SOW page (I4) and its Word/Markdown export do not show the benchmark yet (I4's `_blocks`). It is a one-line addition if wanted.
