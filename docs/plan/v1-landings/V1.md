## What landed
- `sh_platform/v1/v1_voice_routes.py` and `v1_voice.permissions.yaml`. Every company role may use these (company_admin, sponsor, analyst, owner, approver, participant):
  - `GET /api/v1/voice` → `{available, note}`
  - `POST /api/v1/voice/transcribe` `{audio (base64 WAV), mime}` → `{text}` or `{failed: true}`
  - `POST /api/v1/voice/speak` `{text}` → `{audio, mime, clipped}` or `{failed: true}`. The text is clipped to 1,200 characters at a sentence end. Names are registered with the vault first, then the text goes out through the provider's egress gateway. Audio is never stored.
  - A malformed body gets a 400. Anything else that goes wrong gets 200 with `failed: true`.
- `sh_platform/v1/file_context.py`:
  - `preview_for_agent(plane, iid, files) -> list[str]`: one flat line per file. A sample gives its columns and up to three rows of values. A document gives its first 1,500 characters. A picture, a PDF or an unreadable file gives `"<name> (document): I can't read this type yet; ask the person to paste the text"`.
  - `added_line(files)` → "I added a.csv, b.txt.".
- **Additive hooks:**
  - `a1_chat.py`: `context(..., files=None)` adds `files_now` (trimmed like `earlier_turns`). `ask(..., files=None)` and `chat(..., files=None)` take the files. An empty message becomes "I added <name>". The person's turn lists the file names in `did`.
  - `a1_routes.py`: `POST /api/v1/build/{iid}/blueprint/chat` also takes `files: [{name, data, kind?}]`. They are saved with `i2_north.save_file`, and the body limit rises to I2's turn limit only when files are present.

## Data locality (read this before a demo)
Speech runs on Gemini's free tier, and only when `SH_MODE=dev` with `GOOGLE_API_KEY` set (A-033). In any other mode `GET /api/v1/voice` says `available: false` and every screen carries on in text. Audio cannot be tokenised. The recording leaves the machine as it is, so **only synthetic demo data and test voices may be used**: never a real client's words. Spoken replies are tokenised before they leave, and names are read as "a colleague" or "the company". The screen note says "Voice runs on a development service: synthetic data only."
