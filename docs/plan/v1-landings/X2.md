## What landed

- `sh_platform/v1/x2_home.py`: `build(plane, viewer)`. Finds intents as `intents/*/thread.json` or `intents/*/sow.json`; reads `thread.json`, `sow.json`, `room_state.json`, `build/scan/<iid>.json`, `build/mapping/<iid>.json`, `build/ops/loaded.json`. Every file may be absent or broken (it then counts as not there). What waits, per seat:
  - Decision maker: thread `open` → "Answer North (n of 6 needs met)" → `/centre/intent/<iid>/thread`; thread `sow_ready` and SOW not signed → "Read and sign" (+ unsettled unclear count) → `/centre/intent/<iid>/sow`; scan with `agreed: null` → "Agree the scope: N of M can be built now" → `/centre/build/<iid>/scan`.
  - Architect: SOW signed, no scan → "Review the design" → `/centre/build/<iid>/design`; scan agreed, no mapping → `/centre/build/<iid>/mapping`; mapping rows `unclear` → "Correct the lines".
  - Data engineer: mapping rows exist and no `loaded.json` entry for any of the SOW's sources → "Run the loads" → `/centre/build/<iid>/runs`.
  - Ops manager: mapping rows exist and a SOW source has no `loaded` entry → "Confirm the period" → `/centre/build/ops`.
  - Consumer: any SOW source `loaded` → "Open Consume" → `/centre/consume` (KPI cards and offers).
  - Per intent: title (SOW title, else first person turn, else id), stage, "Nira: waiting on X since D" from `room_state.json`, links to the thread or SOW and to the room.
- `sh_platform/v1/x2_routes.py`: `GET /api/v1/home` → `{name, seats: [{id, name}], may_start, waiting: [...], intents: [...]}`. Super admin (no company) gets 403.
- `sh_platform/v1/x2.permissions.yaml`: `GET /api/v1/home` for the six company roles.
- `ui/src/v1/home/routes.tsx` (`home`), `HomePage.tsx`: title "What waits for you, <first name>", seat chips, "Start an intent" (decision maker, goes to I3's `/centre/intent/new`), the waiting cards, the intents list, footnote. Five states through `ScreenState`; cards stack at phone width.
- Tests: `tests/v1/test_x2.py` (2 passed: Arjun sees thread/sign/scope/mapping/loaded/consume, Leela sees no sign, Dev sees consume only, a broken `sow.json` gives a line and no 500, Fresh is empty, super admin 403). `ui/src/v1/home/home.test.tsx` (2 passed: populated and refused). `npx tsc --noEmit` clean.
