# K1 landing: the Map (`/centre/map`)

## What it is
Four read-only tabs, all generated from records (nothing drawn by hand):
- **Data flow**: Sources, Files, Raw data (bronze), Company model (silver), Ready for reports (gold), Dashboards, People. Click a box for owner, last load, status (gold), what feeds it and what it feeds. `?focus=<box id>` opens a box already chosen.
- **Process maps**: every signed intent's swim lane, drawn with `ui/src/v1/i6/Swimlane.tsx`. A project the person may only preview shows the title and no steps.
- **Definitions**: `k2_map.concepts` (agreed meaning, variants, owner, version).
- **Health** (data team): per source, late (days), files turned away, values older than 90 days, values not yet verified, open breaks. Counts that need warehouse rows follow E1d; with nothing visible they read "Not available yet", never 0.

## Files
Backend: `sh_platform/v1/k3_map.py`, `k3_routes.py`, `k3.permissions.yaml`. Routes `GET /api/v1/centre-map/{flow,processes,definitions,health}` (all company roles; handlers filter).
UI: `ui/src/v1/k3/` (MapPage, FlowTab, ProcessTab, DefinitionsTab, HealthTab, api.ts, dates.ts, routes.tsx, fixtures.json, k3.test.tsx).
Shell: one Map entry in `ui/src/shell/copy.ts` (centres) and one line in `ui/src/shell/roles.ts` (`barFor`: Map for analyst and company_admin). Backups: `.sh-tmp/copy.ts.bak-v4`, `.sh-tmp/roles.ts.bak-v4`.
Tests: `tests/v1/test_k3.py` (13), `ui/src/v1/k3/k3.test.tsx` (12). `fixtures.json` is real handler output; `test_k3.py` checks its keys against the handlers.

## Access rules
- Data team (analyst, company_admin): every layer. Everyone else: only gold tables of projects they may open (G1), dashboards they may view (`cx_dash.may_view`) and the people on those; no sources, files, raw or model tables, no health.
- A restricted project's boxes, lane and definitions are not sent to a person off the team.

## How to see it
1. Sign in as an analyst or company admin: MAP is on the top bar after CONSUME. Open `/centre/map`.
2. Data flow: pick a box. Process maps: choose a signed intent. Health: late sources show "Late by N days".
