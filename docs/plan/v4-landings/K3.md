# K3 Manager inbox (UC3) and manager research (UC7)

## What changed
- Action > Managers (`/centre/action/managers`): per manager and fund, the statement expected for the last month end, due date from the signed `statement_due` rule, received, late (days), revised count, last value and its status word. "Draft a follow-up" gives a plain-text email to copy. Nothing is ever sent.
- Consume > Managers (`/centre/consume/managers`, `/centre/consume/managers/:managerId`): funds, commitments (called and uncalled), statements timeline with revisions, notes and meetings, open questions, diligence documents (library, access-filtered), and "What changed since the last meeting?" with dated lines and source chips plus document passages. Rules-worded; never says a manager is good or bad.
- A manager is the `manager_name` on its funds; the id in the URL is a slug of it. A fund is visible when one of its valuations sits in a portfolio the person may see (E1d); administrators see all.
- Notes live in `data/<company>/managers/<id>/notes.json` (created on first note; a drafted follow-up is logged there quietly).

## How to see it (demo)
1. Sign in as an analyst or admin; open Action, then the Managers tab.
2. A manager whose statement is past its due date shows "Late n days"; press "Draft a follow-up", copy the text.
3. Click the manager's name. Add a meeting note with a date, then press "Show what changed".
4. Sign in as a person with a grant on one portfolio only: only that portfolio's managers appear; other manager URLs say they cannot be found.

## Known limits
- Commitments come from K4's `k6_liquidity.commitments()`: only confirmed, non-superseded calls count as called. A company whose warehouse predates K4's model change still says "Not available yet".
- Earlier missing statements are looked for back six month ends (`MAX_BACK`), from the fund's first statement (or first value if it has no statement).
- Cadence (monthly or quarterly) is inferred from the fund's statement history; a new fund is treated as monthly.
- "What changed" is worded by rules (no model call) and says so (`worded_by: rules`); `skills/lumen/managers.md` is ready for a model phrasing step if wanted.
- Day counts use the company's local date of receipt.

## Fixes after review
- Commitments: `_commitments` now uses `k6_liquidity.commitments()` (confirmed, non-superseded calls only; an unconfirmed, cancelled or corrected call no longer counts) and keeps the E1d entity visibility filter. Commitment, called and uncalled go through `_money()`: 5000000, 0, 5000000 (no `5000000.00000000` or `0E-8`). Test with a real commitment, noticed, cancelled, confirmed and corrected calls, and a person without access.
- "What changed": the valuation line uses thousands separators ("5,100,000 USD"); a value restated for an older date but recorded after the meeting is now included (keyed on `recorded_at` too, line says "recorded 8 Oct 2026"). `last_value` gained `recorded_at`.
- Pills read "6 days late" and "Received 2 days late".
- Inbox lists every outstanding earlier month end (`earlier_missing` per fund, `counts.missing_earlier`) with its signed due date; the follow-up draft chases them too, and the button is on when any exist. Quarterly funds only count quarter ends.
- `resolve_question` records `answered_by` and `answered_at`.
- Tests: 12 python (3 new: commitments, earlier missing, restated value), 8 vitest (pill texts updated). UI fixtures were not regenerated; the new fields are optional in `api.ts`.
