# K4 · Commitments and capital calls (UC6)

## What changed
- Core model: new table `commitments` (id, organization_id, legal_entity_id, instrument_id, source_record_id, commitment_amount, currency, committed_on,
  recorded_at, supersedes_commitment_id); `cash_movements` gained `due_date`, `notice_kind` (call, distribution, recallable), `commitment_id`.
  Regenerated `docs/v2/evidence-to-action-mvp.{dbml,duckdb.sql,postgres.sql}` (core is now 42 tables).
- `e0a_catalogue.reload_core(plane)` (called from `version_of`): a company made before this release gets a new approved version
  ("Product release: ...") with what the core now has and it lacks; add-only, company extension kept, warehouse migrated on next connect.
- `e2_extract.py`: a capital call or distribution notice is recognised; extra fields `notice_kind`, `fund`, `due_date`, `distribution_amount`
  (plus the existing `call_amount`, `currency`) each with page and line. The amount waits for a person. Statements read exactly as before.
  Reading records a `noticed` cash movement per commitment of the fund; confirming (or correcting) the amount records a `confirmed` one that
  supersedes it, linked to the commitment. A notice for a fund with no commitment: nothing on read, plain refusal on confirm.
- `k6_liquidity.py`: `record_commitment`, `commitments`, `record_notice`, `alert_if_due`, `forecast(plane, as_of, cutoff, viewer, currency)`.
  Confirmed call due within signed rule `cash_call_alert_days` (parameter `days`, default 30) opens Finance case "Fund the capital call"
  (type `capital_call`, accounting view affected); a later notice for the same fund and portfolio joins the open case.
- Tests: `tests/v1/test_k6.py` (15 tests).

## Forecast shape (for K2)
`forecast(...) -> {as_of, cutoff, entities: [{entity_id, entity_code, entity_name, currency, available_cash, cash_sources[{position_id, portfolio_id,
position_date, status, amount}], buckets[{key: overdue|30|60|90, label, calls, distributions, cash_after_calls (None for overdue), items[{id, kind, recallable,
fund, instrument_id, commitment_id, due_date, days, amount, own_amount, own_currency, status, confirmed, source_record_id}]}], uncalled, unconfirmed[items],
problems[str]}]}`. All Decimal. Cash = latest cash-instrument position at or before as_of (administrator view first). A figure with no signed FX rate is left
out and named in `problems`. `viewer` filters entities through E1d.

## How to see it (demo company)
1. Restart the backend (orchestrator): opening the warehouse reloads the core, adds the table and columns.
2. In Python: `k6_liquidity.record_commitment(plane, {...})` for a fund, then register a notice text ("Capital call notice", "Fund name:", "Due date:", "Capital required: USD ...") through intake.
3. Evidence screen: the notice shows notice kind, due date and amount with page and line; confirm the amount.
4. Action > cases: "Fund the capital call" appears if due within 30 days. `k6_liquidity.forecast(plane, today)` shows it in the 30-day bucket.

## Known limits
- A notice is shared across commitments of the fund by commitment size (one notice has no investor field). Per-investor notices need an investor line.
- A call past its due date stays in "overdue" until superseded or cancelled (no payment matching yet).
- With several portfolios in one entity and no valuation of the fund, the first portfolio by code is used.

## Fixes after review
- Blocker 1 (`e2_extract.py`): `_CALL` is now `capital call(?!ed)|...`, so "Total capital called to date" in a statement is no longer a notice. Test `test_capital_called_to_date_in_a_statement_is_not_a_notice` (statement with that line and a due date: no `notice_kind`, no cash movement).
- Estimated split (`k6_liquidity.py`): forecast items carry `estimated` (True when the fund has more than one commitment, so the per-entity amount is shared by commitment size, not read from the notice). K2 should show "estimated" on those lines.
- `e0a_catalogue.reload_core`: a product release no longer supersedes open human proposals. Only the old current version is superseded; `approve()` refuses a stale proposal with its existing "propose it again" message.
- `unconfirmed` now lists every noticed call (sorted by due date), including ones beyond 90 days. Test `test_a_far_unconfirmed_call_is_still_flagged_and_a_shared_notice_is_marked_estimated`.
- Notice supersede lookup in `record_notice` matches on `source_record_id` only, so a second document with the same fund, kind and due date adds its own movement.
- Cash ranking (accounting view above operational) is left as designed; `cash_sources` carries `position_date` and `status`.
- Tests: `tests/v1/test_k6.py` 17 passed. Run with test_e0a, test_d1, test_e2*: 36 passed, 5 failed, all on the stale 41-table count (orchestrator updates to 42).
