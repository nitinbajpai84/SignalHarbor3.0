## How it works
- `benchmark_return(plane, code, start, end, cutoff, currency)`: opening and closing index levels (`market_observations`, type `index_level`, known by the cutoff, corrections respected) per component of the definition in force; index return in the reporting currency (levels converted by the signed `fx_fixing` at their own dates); plus `spread_bps` a year pro rata for the days; weighted by the weights in force at the start date (must add to 1). Returns `{value, definition{approval_status..}, components[], problems[]}`; value is None with a plain reason when anything is missing or a level is more than 7 days older than the date.
- `portfolio_return(...)`: Modified Dietz from `portfolio_value` at both dates and confirmed calls, subscriptions (in) and distributions, redemptions (out), day-weighted. Returns `{value, opening, closing, flows, statuses, problems}`.
- `k7_benchmark.certified_return`: refuses (409, plain words) unless the definition is `approved`; a draft or `in_progress` one opens one `policy_question` case ("Agree the definition of benchmark X"), not one per call. `k7_benchmark.comparison(plane, portfolio_id, code, start, end, cutoff, currency)` gives `{portfolio, benchmark, difference, certified, problems}` for the Consume tile.
- Span: a KPI whose name, definition or formula mentions "benchmark" adds `gold_<slug>_benchmark_monthly` (month, portfolio_return, benchmark_return, difference); passes the plan validator.
