# K6 · Limits and trade checks (UC5, UC8)

## What changed
- `sh_platform/v1/e3_recon.py`: two new checks, `limits` and `trade_vs_admin`, served by the existing routes (`POST /api/v1/recon/{code}/run`, `GET /api/v1/recon/runs`). Run records gain `rule_words` (the tolerance or limit in plain words) and, for limits, `causes` and `limit_rule_version_ids`.
- `limits`: every signed rule of type `limit` ({dimension: asset_class|manager|liquidity|single_name, bucket, min_pct, max_pct}, percent) against every portfolio: latest valuation per instrument as known at the cutoff (rejected ignored), converted to the portfolio currency, share of the portfolio total. Breach opens a case typed `limit_breach`; the cause (in the case description and the run record) is `classification` (a holding has no value for the dimension), else `stale data` (an input older than 90 days or still proposed), else `real breach`. Needs no cash tolerance; refuses with a plain sentence when no limit is signed.
- `trade_vs_admin`: last 30 days of `transactions`, split by the source system type (`administrator` against `OMS` or `trading`), matched by `external_trade_id`, then by instrument, date and currency with the amount within the signed `cash_tolerance`. Different amount, instrument, currency or date, and unmatched trades on either side, open `break` cases (titles "Trade differs ..." / "Trade not matched ...").
- `ui/src/v1/a5/ReconPage.tsx`: lists the two new checks (kept locally so the shared `CHECKS` in `api.ts` is untouched), shows the rule in words and the cause of each limit breach.
- Tests: `tests/v1/test_e3b.py` (17 tests, 10 new), new `ui/src/v1/a5/ReconPage.test.tsx` (1 test).

## See it on screen
1. Sign sample limits (rule type `limit`) and a cash tolerance in Settings, load valuations and trades.
2. Action > Reconciliations: the cards "Limits" and "Trades against the administrator".
3. Run now: breaches open cases; the card lists the signed rule in words and each breach with its cause.

## Known limits
- Liquidity: private, real estate and real asset classes or types count as illiquid, everything else liquid (no liquidity column exists).
- Classification cause is literal to the card: any holding in the portfolio without the attribute.
- The 30-day window ends on the chosen day; trade breaks dedupe on case title (instrument, date, trade id).
