INTENT OF WORK · VERSION 1

Monthly performance against benchmark for the family council

Drafted by North with Mei Chen
01

Purpose

A monthly page for the family council showing how the liquid portfolio did against the office benchmark, what drove the difference, and any figure that changed after the report went out.

02

Who reads it

The family council. Owned by Daniel Foo, CIO. Raj Patel (Finance) approves each month; Sofia Reyes (Risk) is on the team.

03

Benchmark

Evergreen Office blend, version 1: global equity index 42%, bond index 28%, private markets sleeve 30% (the equity index plus 3% a year). Approved from 1 Jan 2026. Version 2 is still being agreed; this report stays on version 1 until it is signed.

04

Questions it answers

How did we do against the blend this month? What drove the gap? Did any manager change a figure after we reported?

05

Measures and figures

Monthly return of the liquid portfolio, the blend and the difference in points. First check, September 2026: 4.17% against 2.71%, ahead by 1.46 points.

06

Sources

The administrator file, the manager statements, the trading export and the index levels.

07

How it is done today

Mei collects the files and builds the page in Excel over two days. Raj converts to SGD and approves. Daniel reads it.

08

Rules and checks

Any change over 100,000 USD or 0.5% of a fund is shown and explained. Example: September was issued at 57,600,000 and is 57,780,000 now (+180,000) because Fund A's final statement is 10,180,000, not 10,000,000.

09

Timing and delivery

Ready by the 5th business day of each month, in Consume and the Morning Note.
