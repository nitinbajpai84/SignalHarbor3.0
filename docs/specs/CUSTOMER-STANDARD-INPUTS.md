# Customer standard inputs

Principle: **Bring what you have. Decide what only you can decide. Sign.**

Agents read what the customer brings and draft everything. They ask only what they could not work out. The inputs below are asked for once, held in one company profile, and read by all three modules. All examples and sample data are for the synthetic "Illustrative Lakeside Family Office".

How to read the tables:

- **Required or helpful:** required means a module cannot draft well without it. Helpful means it makes the draft better or saves a question.
- **Accepted forms:** Excel, CSV, PDF, Word, email export, link or system access.
- **Used in:** module, step, agent. M1 is the Intent Centre (North), M2 is the Baseline (Lens), M3 is Assess and plan (Lens). Nira uses the people list in every module.
- **If missing:** either the agent asks (one short ask to the right person), or the item is marked "Not available yet" and the draft goes on.
- **Interview question it can replace:** the question in "The one interview" (below) that a good document makes unnecessary or shorter.

## The one interview

**One interview, then only approvals.** Each person is interviewed once, by North and Lens, in a conversation of 10 to 15 minutes. The agents prefill from the documents above and ask only the gaps. The answers feed all three modules.

People come back only to:

1. approve a signed output;
2. answer at most 3 exception questions;
3. take part in a later process-improvement round, which is a short delta interview each quarter.

### Leaders and participants (about 10 minutes, North)

| # | Question | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | "Which decisions do you make each month that need better information?" | charter problems (M1) |
| Q2 | "What slows you down or goes wrong today? Who is affected, and how often?" | pain points (M1), with severity |
| Q3 | "Which numbers tell you it is going well, and where do you see them today?" | measures (M1/M2), the report that holds them (M2 source), and the reported figure as the benchmark (M3) |
| Q4 | "What would you like to see coming before it happens?" | future signals (M1), outside feeds to consider (M3) |
| Q5 | "If you could change one thing, what would you change, and which number should move?" | what-if levers (M1) |
| Q6 | "What matters now, and what can wait?" | priority Must/Should/Could (M1), impact score (M3) |

### Owners of a number or a process (about 15 minutes, Lens)

Lens shows its draft first and the owner confirms or corrects.

| # | Question | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Q7 | "Where does this number come from, who produces it, and when does it arrive?" | source, owner, arrival and SLA (M2 catalogue) |
| Q8 | "How is it worked out today?" | business and technical definition (M2 glossary and technical definition), the rule for the trial (M3) |
| Q9 | "Who may see it? Does it hold personal data?" | governance per field (M2), restricted handling (M3) |
| Q10 | "Who do you get inputs from, and who uses your output?" | process map lanes and hand-offs (M2) |
| Q11 | "Can you share one recent full period of the data, and the figure you reported for it?" | sample (M3 scan and trial), benchmark (M3) |
| Q12 | "Is anything blocking access: licences, systems, or work already under way?" | blockers and running programmes (M3 plan bands) |

### Sponsor (about 5 minutes)

| # | Question | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Q13 | "What should be true in three months, who signs each result, and by when?" | success criteria, sign-off names, deadlines (all modules) |

### Everyone, last

| # | Question | Feeds |
|---|---|---|
| Q14 | "Is there anything else you would like us to know, anything we did not ask?" | A note kept in the person's own words. Lens and North file it to the right module as an extra pain point, signal, lever, risk or context. It also asks whether the note may be shared, named or anonymous, following the session's names rule |

Q14 closes every interview, for leaders, owners and the sponsor.

## Follow-up questions the agents may ask

These are rules, not a fixed script. North and Lens may think of a follow-up on their feet, but only when one of these triggers fires.

| Trigger | Example follow-up |
|---|---|
| Vague answer | Ask for one example or a number. "Can you give me one recent example?" or "Roughly how often: weekly or monthly?" |
| Contradicts a document | Quote the source and ask which is right. "The monthly pack shows 6.8%; you said about 7. Which should we use?" |
| A new name, system or file appears | Ask who owns it and how it arrives. That adds a source or owner |
| Strong pain | Ask for the cost and who feels it. "What does that cost you each month, in time or money?" |
| A number differs from the benchmark | Ask whether it is a different definition or a different period |
| Hinted dependency | Ask "Who gives you that, and who uses what you send?" |
| Restricted topic (pay, HR) | Do not dig. Note it and ask whether a named person should cover it separately |

Limits:

- At most 2 follow-ups per question.
- Never ask what the documents already answer.
- Stop when the person signals they are done.
- Every follow-up and its answer is tagged to the question it came from, so it reaches the right output.

## Exact outputs

### Signed charter (M1)

- Problems, each with: priority, "So that" line, votes.
- Pain points: who, how often, severity.
- Future signals: what, possible source.
- Levers: change, the measure it moves.
- The people who took part.
- The signature.

### Signed baseline (M2)

- Per measure:
  - name;
  - business definition;
  - technical definition (sources, tables, fields, calculation, filters, period, currency);
  - business owner, data owner and process owner;
  - tracked today: yes or no;
  - personal data, and who may see it.
- The process map per measure.
- The data catalogue: Sources, Tables, Fields, Extractions, KPIs.
- The business glossary.
- Known risks (accepted ambiguities).
- Left-out measures, with reasons.
- Signatures.

### Signed phased plan (M3)

- Source map: impact, readiness, zone, reasons.
- Scan results: columns found, fill, date range, gaps.
- Trial per measure: our figure, your figure, matches / gap / cannot, likely reason.
- Findings: ready / unclear / unknown.
- Risks, with decisions.
- Plan bands:
  - Now, 0-3 weeks: measures delivered, pain points resolved, effort, team;
  - Next, 3-8 weeks: blocked by, who unblocks, date;
  - Later, 8+ weeks: why, what it affects, programme.
- Signatures.

## Referring to inputs during the session

- Every draft an agent shows names the input it came from, for example: "From: Monthly investment committee pack, Sept, page 3". The person can open the source and check it.
- Nira shows a readiness meter, for example: "Lens has read 7 of 9 inputs; it can draft about 80% of the baseline".
- Missing inputs become one short ask, sent to the right person. Nira sends it, reminds the person, and shows the answer to everyone who needs it.
- Answers from the interview are tagged the same way, for example: "From: your interview, Q7".

## Privacy

- Inputs stay on the customer's machine.
- Agents see only masked passages, column names and counts, under the egress rules.
- Restricted documents (pay, HR) are read only where allowed, and otherwise left unread and marked "Not available yet".
- Personal data is never copied into drafts. Where a note from Q14 is shared, it follows the session's names rule: named or anonymous, as the person chose.
