How they read:
- **55**: the window is the as-of date (latest position date) to 14 days after it (the card's 14 days). A notice with an ex-date or a pay date in it and no election is a booking at the holding and cash the notice states (`quantity_held`, `expected_cash` fields); one with an election deadline in it is a decision, its last price the position's value over quantity at the as-of date. An election past its deadline is `missed`, never a decision.
- **65**: latest appraisal of each asset on or before the as-of date (RA-01's 2024 appraisal is history); SGD at the latest fixing; the deed's holder beside the owning entity.
- **66**: as-of date less the latest appraisal date, due above 365 days (the card's line, as card 21 used its card's 90).
- **67**: the custodian's lot register at 30 Sep, each lot at the holding's own 30 Sep price. The single-name limit and the exemption of the sovereign note are the office's signed rules: the recipe reads the exemption from `rule_versions` (so HTN29, 7.08 percent of the book, is not ranked), the signal reads the limit. The sale is `(holding - limit x book) / (1 - limit)`, the book unchanged by a sale.
- **69**: each fund's latest holdings list (a newer list replaces the old one; tested) times its latest statement value, the same value `expected_distributions` uses (an amended statement wins; Cedarpoint Opportunities II still rests on 31 Mar and Cedarpoint Ventures III and Saltmarsh Renewables I on 30 Jun, shown as `oldest_value`). The fund keys (A, C, F) are the codes the managers' list writes, read from the row's source record. The liquid book is what the portfolios with the signed single-name limit hold at their latest position date (13 Oct), as the single-name limit measures it; family_investments adds the latest value of every fund (cash out).
- **68**: the income calendar's expected dividend and coupon lines paid after the as-of date and up to a year after it (the card's 12 months). It is the office's calendar figure, not a rate typed here; cash already received is not forecast (tested).

### Things the build found

- **The notices and the calendar agree.** The cash each of the ten bookings' notices states equals the income calendar's line of the same security and pay date (tested). Card 55 reads the notices, card 68 the calendar; they are two sources of one figure.
- **The key's total gain is net.** `total_unrealised_gain` (4,030,570) is the gain of all the lots with the losses taken off; the lots with a gain alone are 5,485,520. The recipe's `total_gain` is the net figure, `total_loss` the losses.
- **Five names are over the 5 percent limit, not one.** At 30 Sep Harborview (5.75), Coastal Rail Holdings (5.64), Larkspur Health (5.46), Juniper Consumer Brands (5.38) and Evermore Banking Group (5.29) are all above it, and Northwind and Atlas just above. The key and the signal name the largest one not exempt, Harborview. Whether the office wants one signal per name is its call.
- **The tender's event text.** The key calls it "voluntary tender offer"; the model stores the action type `tender_offer` and the election flag. The test compares the type.
- **The card's own formula for 68** (coupon rate times face plus dividend per share times quantity) is what the reader checks each calendar line against (it flags a line that differs; none does). The recipe keeps the calendar figure.
