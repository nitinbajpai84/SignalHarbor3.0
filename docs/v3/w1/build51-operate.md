## Left unbuilt on purpose
- No recipe or signal was written for the eight cards above: nothing could be proved on the real readers, and the rules forbid inventing a table, a rule or a threshold. The stubs `position_break`, `price_gap_pct`, `unsettled_trades` and `source_quality` stay stubs (tested).
- The cards carry no `signals` rows, so the signals of the two built cards are my own reading of each card's story; each has an exception signal with a hold (`accept_administrator_trades` holds accepting the administrator's trades until the break is raised; `settle_forward` holds settling or rolling a forward whose confirmation differs or is missing). The callers that must honour the two holds do not exist yet (same open point as the top 50, `build-operate.md` Needs 10).
- `fx_mtm` does not join the Trust's cash to compute the shortfall; the test shows the two figures give the key's number. A signal that reads two figures (forward USD to deliver against entity cash) waits for the engine's multi-figure input.
- Signal events: the signals trigger on `figure_changed` with one event per row of the figure, as in the top 50 (`build-operate.md` Needs 9).
