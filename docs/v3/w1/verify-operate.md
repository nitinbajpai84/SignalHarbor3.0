## Five worst

1. Card 27: cash breaks in every currency are tested against one USD 5,000 tolerance. SGD breaks can be called over or inside tolerance wrongly. The test filters to USD and hides it.
2. Card 27: a missing bank statement makes false breaks. The recipe has no guard, and the test skips those days itself.
3. Card 1: the signal that holds a notice compares against a field no recipe produces, with no null guard. A notice with no table amount can be held.
4. Card 16: the test asserts a typed 6 batches on the old name, the key says 5, and the count takes an arbitrary mapping across sheets and sources.
5. Card 19: the role build checks "63" (9 Oct), not the 13 Oct figure of 65. The check passes on a stale number.

Not checked: the signal engine itself (on the `sh3-signals` branch, not in this repo), so engine null-comparison behaviour is assumed to match Python. The hold names have no caller in the repo.
