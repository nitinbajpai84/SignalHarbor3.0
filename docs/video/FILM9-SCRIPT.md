About 3.5 minutes, narrated. Signal Harbor 3.0 through ten people in a family office, from the CIO to the clerk, and how each one
works with Harbor in its four places: Action, Build, Consume and Knowledge.

- Made by `tools/video/film9.py` (voice, cards, takes, cut); builds that need drafting are started and waited out by `tools/video/film9_stage.py`.
- Recorded from the running app at http://127.0.0.1:8640 against the film office `D:/Temp/sh3-film` (synthetic data only), each person signed in as themselves.
- Every figure said or shown is the one on screen. Drafting takes minutes: the brief shots are recorded once Harbor has drafted, and the narration says so.
- Outputs in `docs/video/out/`: `signal-harbor-a-day-at-evergreen.mp4`, `-share.mp4` (under 20 MB), `-poster.png`, `.srt`.
- Look: the 3.0 tokens (navy #1B3A5C, brass #B08D57, light #F4F6F9; Fraunces and Instrument Sans). Lower thirds carry the hour, the name and the title.
