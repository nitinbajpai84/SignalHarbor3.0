# UC01: A capital call arrives

About 2.5 minutes, narrated. One capital call at Evergreen Office, a family office, from the notice to the money arriving and everyone seeing it (docs/v3/use-cases/UC01-CAPITAL-CALL.md). Synthetic names and figures only.

- Made by `tools/video/uc01.py` (voice, cards, cut, sound, subtitles, outputs). Cards only: no app recording.
- Look: the 3.0 palette (navy #1B3A5C, amber #B7791F, green #1E7B4F, red #B23B32, brass #B08D57, slate #5A6878) on the light ground #F4F6F9. Fraunces and Instrument Sans.
- The recurring visual is the signal table: three columns, what arrives, the signal Harbor catches, and who acts and how little. A row lights up as Harbor catches its signal, and its action pill turns amber (waiting for you) then green (done).
- The lines under each scene are the narration, as said. "Shows" is what the picture shows.

- Kind: card
- Shows: "A fund asks for money." Large, navy, on the light ground. Below it, small: "Evergreen Office · Capital call 7".

> A fund asks for money.
> This is one capital call at Evergreen Office, a family office, from the first email to the money arriving.

### Scene 2: The notice arrives
- Kind: card
- Shows: the signal table, row 1 lights: what arrives "Capital call notice No. 7, Cedarpoint Ventures III"; the signal "USD 1,250,000 due 23 Oct"; who acts "Sam Lee: Log it", amber, then green when Sam taps Log.

> On Tuesday the thirteenth of October, a notice arrives in the operations inbox.
> Cedarpoint Ventures III asks Evergreen Holdings for {USD 1,250,000|one million, two hundred and fifty thousand US dollars}, due on the twenty third.
> Harbor reads every field, and checks each one twice.
> Sam Lee needs one tap: Log.

### Scene 3: It fits the commitment
- Kind: card
- Shows: the signal table, row 2 lights: what arrives "Commitment: 10,000,000"; the signal "Unfunded after this call: 2,750,000"; who acts "Helen Foo: Record it", amber, then green.

> Harbor checks the call against the commitment.
> Evergreen Holdings committed ten million, and {2,750,000|two million, seven hundred and fifty thousand} is still unfunded after this call.
> The call fits, so Helen Foo records it with one click.

### Scene 4: Can we pay it?
- Kind: card
- Shows: the signal table, row 3 lights: what arrives "Cash by entity, verified today"; the signal "Short USD 270,000 after the 250,000 buffer"; who acts "Daniel Foo: decides", amber.

> Can we pay it? Harbor reads the cash in each entity, and keeps the 250,000 buffer the office rule asks for.
> Holdings has {USD 1,230,000|one million, two hundred and thirty thousand dollars} in US dollars, so it is {short USD 270,000|two hundred and seventy thousand dollars short}.
> It can cover that by converting {SGD 361,800|three hundred and sixty one thousand, eight hundred Singapore dollars}.

### Scene 5: Daniel decides
- Kind: card
- Shows: the signal table, row 3 turns green "Decided"; row 4 lights: what arrives "Plan: convert SGD 361,800 on Thursday"; the signal "Convert to USD, pay on the 23rd"; who acts "Daniel Foo: Approve", amber, then green with a seal.

> Daniel Foo, head of finance, sees the plan: convert on Thursday, and pay on the twenty third.
> A payment over 500,000 needs his approval, so he approves it. The record keeps his name and the time.

### Scene 6: The bank account changed
- Kind: card
- Lower third: none
- Shows: the whole frame in red #B23B32. "Account ending 2209" struck through, then "ending 4471" large in white. Under it: "Payment on hold. Call back on a known number before paying." Then, once the call is named, "Confirmed by call-back, 13 Oct, 15:40."

> Before a payment is prepared, Harbor compares the bank details with the standing instructions.
> The account has changed, from ending 2209 to ending 4471.
> Harbor holds the payment, and does not suggest the new account.
> The office rule says: call back on a known number before paying.
> Grace Ong phones Cedarpoint on the number from the April subscription pack, and they confirm it: they moved banks.

### Scene 7: FX and payment
- Kind: card
- Shows: three steps on a line. Thursday 22 Oct: "Trade matched, 1.3405, within 0.1% of plan", green. Friday 23 Oct: "Payment released by Daniel", green. Monday 26 Oct: "Statement: USD 1,250,000 out. Call 7 closed", green. The signal table's rows 4 and 5 are green.

> On Thursday, the currency trade matches the approved plan, at 1.3405.
> On Friday, Daniel releases the payment.
> On Monday, the bank statement shows the money out, and call seven closes itself.

### Scene 9: Closing
- Kind: card
- Shows: the signal table, all five rows lit and green. Then the closing line, large, and the brand mark "Signal Harbor 3.0".
