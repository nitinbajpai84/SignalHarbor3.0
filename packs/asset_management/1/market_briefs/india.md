# India market brief -- asset management

Dated: 2026-10-02 (prepared for V3-13; no figures included, see status note above).

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) regulates mutual funds and asset management
companies. (Source: builder's general knowledge of SEBI's role; to be confirmed against SEBI's
own publications.)

The Association of Mutual Funds in India (AMFI) is the industry body for mutual funds. It
publishes regular industry data on assets and flows, and it registers mutual fund distributors.
(Source: builder's general knowledge; to be confirmed against AMFI's own publications.)

Funds in this market are offered in a direct plan and a regular plan; the regular plan's costs
include the distributor's commission. This is the local form of the share-class mix question in
problems.yaml T-CMO-04. (Source: builder's general knowledge; to be confirmed.)

performance figure for India. None was retrieved or cited this session.
