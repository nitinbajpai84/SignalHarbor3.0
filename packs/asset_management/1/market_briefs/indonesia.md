# Indonesia market brief -- asset management

Dated: 2026-10-02 (prepared for V3-13; no figures included, see status note above).

capital market, including investment managers and mutual funds (reksa dana). (Source: builder's
general knowledge of OJK's role; to be confirmed against OJK's own publications.)

Mutual funds are sold through banks and other selling agents. (Source: builder's general
knowledge; a practitioner should confirm how far this holds for the company in
