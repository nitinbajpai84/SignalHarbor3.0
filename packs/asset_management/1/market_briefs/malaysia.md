# Malaysia market brief -- asset management

Dated: 2026-10-02 (prepared for V3-13; no figures included, see status note above).

The Securities Commission Malaysia (SC) regulates the capital market, including fund management
companies and unit trust funds. (Source: builder's general knowledge of the SC's role; to be
confirmed against the SC's own publications.)

The Federation of Investment Managers Malaysia (FIMM) is the industry's self-regulatory body for
the marketing and distribution of unit trusts and private retirement schemes. (Source: builder's
general knowledge; to be confirmed against FIMM's own publications.)

Unit trusts are sold through agents and banks in this market. (Source: builder's general
knowledge; a practitioner should confirm how far this holds for the company in question.)
