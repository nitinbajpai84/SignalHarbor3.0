# Singapore market brief -- asset management

Dated: 2026-10-02 (prepared for V3-13; no figures included, see status note above).

The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) is Singapore's central bank and financial regulator.
It licenses and supervises fund management companies, and it publishes an annual survey of the
Singapore asset management industry. (Source: builder's general knowledge of MAS's role; to be
confirmed against MAS's own publications.)

The Investment Management Association of Singapore (IMAS) is the industry body for investment
managers in Singapore. (Source: builder's general knowledge; to be confirmed against IMAS's own
publications.)

The Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) governs how client personal data may be collected and
used, including for client engagement and marketing. (Source: builder's general knowledge; the
same Act is named in templates/insurance/1/market_briefs/singapore.md.)
