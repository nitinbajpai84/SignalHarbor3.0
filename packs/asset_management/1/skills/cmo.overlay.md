# Asset management overlay for the `cmo` skill (relabelled "Head of Distribution" in this
# template's roles/role_labels.yaml -- the underlying skill slug stays `cmo`, per design section
# 2.4).

- Vocabulary: AUM, net flows, fee yield, NAV, benchmark, client retention.
- Typical sources: `awm_instruments`, `awm_portfolios`, `awm_positions`.
- Typical KPIs: AUM, net flows, fee yield, performance vs benchmark.
- What "good" looks like: AUM growth driven by net flows, not just market movement; fee yield
  holding or improving as AUM grows.
