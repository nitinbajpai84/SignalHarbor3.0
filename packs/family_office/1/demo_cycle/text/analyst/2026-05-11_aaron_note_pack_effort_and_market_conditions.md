# Note: pack effort and market conditions

Aaron Lyle, analyst. 11 May 2026. For Janet and the committee.

At the April meeting I said the last two packs felt harder because markets were volatile. I have put the numbers side by side.

| Month | Pack hours (effort log) | Market volatility index, month average |
|---|---|---|
| 2025-09 | 48.75 | 17.2 |
| 2025-10 | 50.00 | 16.4 |
| 2025-11 | 50.75 | 15.8 |
| 2025-12 | 51.50 | 16.9 |
| 2026-01 | 48.50 | 18.3 |
| 2026-02 | 52.00 | 20.1 |
| 2026-03 | 55.50 | 31.8 |
| 2026-04 | 55.00 | 29.4 |

Over these eight months the two move together closely (correlation about 0.9). The two highest effort months are the two most volatile.

My reading: volatile months are harder. There is more commentary to write, more values move, and more questions come back from the committee. If that holds, we should plan for it: start the pack two days earlier when volatility is above about 25, and have a second person on the valuation pages.

Caveats: eight months is a short series. I have not split the hours by task.

Aaron
