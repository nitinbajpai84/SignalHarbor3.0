# Policy exception note: USD hedging

Grace Ward, compliance. 14 August 2026.

What the policy says: USD exposure is hedged at 70 to 90 percent with monthly forwards. Until April we ran at about 80 percent.

What has happened: the last USD forward matured on 30 April. The committee agreed on 16 April to pause for one month pending a review of the hedge ratio. The review has been carried forward at the May, June and July meetings. Since 1 May the USD position has been fully open: about 530 million USD at the end of July, bank balances and holdings together, and it has grown with coupons and distributions.

Why now: SGD has strengthened sharply this month. The loss on the open position will be material.

Status: exception logged from 1 May 2026. To be resolved by the committee. I will table this note on 20 August.

Grace
