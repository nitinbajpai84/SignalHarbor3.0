# Quiet end to the third quarter

Dated: 2025-09-30.

Regional equities ended September little changed. Singapore dollar steady at about 1.28 to 1.29 per USD. Short-term SGD deposit rates on operating accounts remain very low while SGD money market funds yield around 1.75 percent. Private market managers report a slower pace of calls than last year.
