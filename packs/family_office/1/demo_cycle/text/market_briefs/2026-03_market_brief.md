# Sharp sell-off, then a partial recovery

Dated: 2026-03-31.

Global equities fell sharply in the first half of March and recovered part of the loss by month end. Volatility measures reached their highest level in over a year, around 30. Regional banks held up better than the wider market. Private market valuations as at 31 March will reflect the fall with the usual lag.
