# SGD jumps; volatility returns

Dated: 2026-08-31.

The Singapore central bank tightened its currency policy in August and the SGD strengthened sharply, from about 1.30 per USD at the start of the month to about 1.23 at the end, one of the largest monthly moves in years. Equity volatility rose to around 30. Investors reporting in SGD with unhedged USD assets took large translation losses; forward hedging demand picked up late in the month.
