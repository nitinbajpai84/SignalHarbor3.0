# Paper for the committee: putting cash above the floor to work

Mei Ling Chong, treasury. 10 March 2026. For the meeting of 19 March.

## Where we are
At the end of February the Holdings SGD account held about 14.5 million and the Shaw Family Trust account about 14.8 million. Against a floor of 2 million each, that is about 25 million above the floor between the two, and it has grown every month since the autumn because distributions have come in faster than calls. Investments sits near its floor and is not part of this paper.

Interest across all our accounts in February was about 3,800.

## Proposal
1. Each Monday, sweep cash above the floor in the Holdings and Trust SGD accounts into the SGD money market funds we already hold: Harbour SGD Liquidity Fund for Holdings, Straits SGD Money Market Fund for the Trust. Leave a working margin of about 1 million above the floor in each account for calls.
2. Redeem from the funds if a call would take an account to the floor. Redemption is next day, which is inside our call notice periods.
3. Report interest monthly to the committee.

## What it needs
- Holdings: a standing instruction under the existing mandate.
- The Trust: a new mandate. The Trust account was never set up for a sweep when the bank offered one in 2024; the trustee has to sign.
- Grace has confirmed the Trust deed permits money market funds.

## What it could earn
The SGD money market funds are paying about 1.75 percent against close to nothing on the operating accounts. On about 24 million that is in the region of 35,000 a month, against under 4,000 today. Illustrative; depends on rates and calls.

Mei Ling
