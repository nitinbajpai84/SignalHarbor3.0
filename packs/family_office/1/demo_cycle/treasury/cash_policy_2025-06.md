# Cash policy, June 2025

Illustrative synthetic document for the Ardmore demo. Approved by the investment committee, June 2025.

1. Each operating entity (E01, E02, E03) keeps a floor of SGD 2,000,000 in its SGD operating account to meet capital calls, taxes and running costs.
2. Cash above the floor is swept to a money market fund (H037 to H040) rather than left in the operating account.
3. The floor is reviewed each June. The head of operations and the treasury lead sign any change.
4. Treasury reports cash above the floor to the committee each month.
5. Capital calls are paid from the operating account within the notice period; a call that would take the account below the floor is raised with the committee before payment.
