# 01 - Pre-read request: what to send before we start

To: Mei Tan, Head of Investments (sponsor), Illustrative Lakeside Family Office
From: the Signal Harbor team

Thank you for agreeing to start. Two short sessions will go better if we have read a few things first. This list is the "ideal pre-read". Send what exists, in whatever state it is in. A rough note is more useful than nothing, and "we do not have one" is a useful answer.

## Three promises

1. **Everything stays on your machine.** The documents are read where they are. Signal Harbor describes your data; it never copies it out of your environment. Files you send as samples stay on your machine and are not uploaded to any outside service.
2. **Descriptions, not data.** If you can, remove real figures and names. Illustrative or masked samples are fine. Never send passwords, keys, or full account numbers.
3. **Nothing is checked against you.** The pre-read is for us to ask better questions. Gaps are findings, not failures.

## The checklist

## What you do not need to send

- Real client or family data. Masked or illustrative is enough.
- Login details of any kind.
- Anything you are not allowed to share. Tell us what exists and we will plan around it.

## Why it matters for the two modules

- Module 1 (intent workshop) uses items 1, 3, 9, 10 and 12 to listen for the real problems and to check them against what is written.
- Module 2 (baseline) uses items 2 to 8 and 11 to map how the data behind those problems is produced today and who touches it.

## Where the files go

Put the files in a folder on your own machine that you choose, for example "ILFO pre-read". Signal Harbor reads from that folder and writes only its own working notes beside it. Tell us if any file must not be opened by the agents and we will mark it as excluded.

## When

If you can send these a few days ahead of the workshop, that is ideal. If only part arrives, we begin anyway; the catalogue will mark the rest as unknown, which is a perfectly good starting point.
