# 02 - Investment Policy Statement

Illustrative Lakeside Family Office (ILFO). Applies to Lakeside Holdings Pte Ltd (E01) and Lakeside Investments Pte Ltd (E02), reported together in SGD. Version 2026.1, in force from 1 January 2026, reviewed once a year by the family council. All amounts are illustrative.

## 1. Objectives

1. Fund the family's approved distributions and the office's running costs without forced sales. Illustrative annual need: distributions SGD 6 million, operating costs SGD 1.2 million.
2. Protect capital against funding shortfalls first. Temporary market volatility is acceptable in the long-term growth sleeve.
3. Grow the real value of the portfolio over the long term through diversified public and private investments.
4. Keep a clear, single picture of what the office owns, owes and can pay.

## 2. Liquidity reserve rule (illustrative)

- **The reserve.** ILFO holds at least SGD 5 million at all times in cash and assets that can be turned into cash within two working days (the "reserve"). The reserve is an additional buffer on top of known obligations. It is not itself spent, and it is not counted twice as an obligation.
- **Six-month headroom test.** Each month, over the next six months: opening liquid resources plus approved inflows, less known obligations, less scenario-only calls, less any proposed new funding, less the reserve. The lowest of the six months is the "minimum six-month funding headroom". Cash is carried forward month to month.
- **Known obligations** are current, unpaid, approved payments: notified capital calls, approved distributions, operating costs. A corrected notice replaces the earlier one; it is not a second payment.
- **Stress case.** Expected future calls from the managers' indicative schedules are multiplied by 1.75 and all estimated fund distributions are removed.
- **Pacing rule.** No new commitment is made if the base-case minimum headroom after it would be below zero. If only the stress case goes below zero, the investment committee decides and records an exception.
- **Currency.** USD calls are funded from USD balances where possible. Buying USD takes two working days to settle.

## 3. Asset allocation ranges (percentage of included assets)

| Sleeve | Range |
|---|---|
| Public equities | 35 to 50 |
| Liquid fixed income | 10 to 25 |
| Private equity and venture (funded value) | 10 to 20 |
| Private credit (funded value) | 3 to 10 |
| Real assets (infrastructure and property, funded value) | 5 to 12 |
| Cash and money market | 5 to 15 |

Unfunded commitments are shown separately and are not counted as assets.

## 4. Concentration and risk limits

- Single issuer, direct holding: not more than 10 per cent of included assets. Review trigger at 5 per cent.
- Single manager: not more than 20 per cent of included assets.
- Look-through exposure to one underlying company (for example a company held through several funds) must be reported to the committee. The policy does not yet set a limit for it.
- Currency: USD is accepted as a home exposure. Any other non-SGD currency exposure that is unhedged may not exceed 10 per cent of included assets.
- Valuation age: private valuations older than 120 days are marked as stale in the pack.

## 5. Who approves what

| Decision | Prepared by | Approves | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|
| This policy and its ranges | Head of Investments | Family council | Investment committee |
| New fund commitment of any size | Head of Investments | Investment committee | Treasurer, Finance Lead |
| Pacing of commitments against the reserve test | Treasurer | Investment committee | Head of Investments |
| Policy exceptions (limit or range breach) | Head of Investments | Investment committee | Risk Analyst |
| Payment of a verified capital call up to USD 1 million | Operations Manager (verifies) | Finance Lead releases, Treasurer co-signs | Head of Investments |
| Payment of a capital call above USD 1 million | Operations Manager (verifies) | Treasurer and Head of Investments approve, Finance Lead releases | Investment committee at next meeting |
| Money-market redemption and FX dealing to fund a call | Treasurer | Treasurer | Finance Lead |
| Change to the manager fee terms recorded by the office | not assigned | not assigned | not assigned |

The last row is deliberately blank: the policy is silent on who keeps and approves the manager fee list.

## 6. Investment committee

The committee meets monthly, on the third Thursday. It receives a pack two working days before. It reviews the funding position, capital calls and unfunded commitments, valuations, exposure, policy exceptions and manager updates, and records decisions in minutes kept by the Operations Manager.

## 7. Review

The policy is reviewed once a year, or after a breach of the reserve rule. The Head of Investments proposes changes. A verbal answer alone is not an approved policy: only the family council can approve a change.
