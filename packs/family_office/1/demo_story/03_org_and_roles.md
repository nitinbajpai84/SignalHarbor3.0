# 03 - Organisation and roles

Illustrative Lakeside Family Office (ILFO) is small. Eight people touch the monthly committee pack, and a ninth (HR) depends on its performance numbers once a quarter. Names are invented; email addresses use example.test.

## Approval authority

See 02_investment_policy.md section 5. In short: the committee approves commitments and exceptions; the Finance Lead releases payments with the Treasurer co-signing; Sara verifies calls but has no payment rights.

## Coverage and gaps

- Sara has no named deputy for the calls mailbox. When she is away the mailbox is not read until she returns, unless someone remembers.
- The Investment Analyst seat has been vacant for seven months. The fee list, manager contact sheet and quarterly report filing were that role's. Only the filing was picked up (by Sara).
- Wei is the only person who can build the exposure workbook. Tom is the only person who builds the stress view.
- The Finance Officer can release payments but does not know the call schedule.
- Lina has no access to the performance numbers except the spreadsheet Finance emails; she cannot check them. Finance, in turn, can open the pay workbook.
- Nobody holds the role of benchmark owner. Mei chose benchmarks informally; Arjun changes them in his workbook when he needs to.
