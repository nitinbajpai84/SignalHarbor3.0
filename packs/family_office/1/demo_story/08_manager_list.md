## Fee terms in words

The fee terms below are what the office understands. The final authority is each fund's agreement and side letter, which the office has but no list quotes from.

- **HPE3.** Management fee of two per cent a year on commitments during the investment period, then one and a half per cent a year on invested cost. Carried interest of twenty per cent over an eight per cent preferred return. Transaction fees are partly offset against the management fee.
- **HPE4.** Management fee of one and three-quarter per cent a year on commitments during the investment period, then one and a half per cent on invested cost. Carried interest of twenty per cent over an eight per cent preferred return.
- **SGF2.** Management fee of two per cent a year on commitments for four years, then on invested cost. Carried interest of twenty per cent over an eight per cent preferred return.
- **IBI1.** One and a quarter per cent a year on invested capital. Carried interest of fifteen per cent over a seven per cent preferred return.
- **ILP2.** One per cent a year on invested capital. Carried interest of fifteen per cent over an eight per cent preferred return. (Wei's workbook says one and a quarter per cent.)
- **KCF2.** One and a quarter per cent a year on invested capital. Performance fee of twelve and a half per cent over a six per cent return. (Sara's tracker says one and a half per cent.)
- **TCV1.** No management fee. Carried interest of ten per cent over an eight per cent preferred return, plus a one-off set-up cost shared among investors. (Wei's workbook shows a one per cent annual fee.)
- **MGE.** Three-quarters of one per cent a year on net asset value. No performance fee. Dealing monthly.
