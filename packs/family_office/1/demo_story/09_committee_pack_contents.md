# 09 - Committee pack contents

The monthly investment committee pack of Illustrative Lakeside Family Office. About fourteen pages, issued by Sara two working days before the third-Thursday meeting. This note says what each section shows, which numbers it uses, where each comes from, and which are typed by hand. KPI names are those of the family_office pack. All amounts in the pack are illustrative.

## Sections

### 1. Cover and agenda (1 page)
Meeting date, attendees, agenda, decisions requested. Typed by Sara; decisions requested typed by Mei.

### 2. Funding position (2 pages)
KPI: Minimum six-month funding headroom (K01).
- Opening cash and liquid resources by account and currency: Northgate statement CSV (S01, T01), after Arjun removes internal transfers between E01 and E02. Source file: Cash_NAV_Consolidation.xlsx.
- Money-market holdings: Northgate positions file (T03).
- Known obligations by month: from the tracker (T08), via Arjun's copy; operating costs and approved distributions from Tom's input tab.
- Expected calls for six months and the stress multiplier (1.75, from the policy): Tom's input tab (MANUAL).
- Reserve (SGD 5 million): policy.
- The headroom by month and the lowest month: formulas in the consolidation workbook, some overwritten by hand.
Pain points: the base case shows only calls already noticed unless Tom adds his own expected view; the stress page is Tom's alone.

### 3. Capital calls and unfunded commitments (2 pages)
KPIs: Known obligations due (K04), Unfunded commitments (K05), Stress funding requirement (K06).
- Calls notified and due in the next six months: tracker Calls tab (typed by Sara from notices).
- Unfunded by commitment: tracker Commitments tab, compared with the Bluewater report's unfunded figure for the four administered funds. Differences are noted by Arjun by hand.
- Stress funding: from the funding page.

### 4. Portfolio value and valuation dates (2 pages)
KPIs: Reported-value evidence coverage (K02), Stale-valuation share (K12).
- Fund NAV: Bluewater NAV report (T04) for four funds; manager PDFs (T07) typed in for the others; Marlowe from the custody positions file.
- Valuation dates and basis: Bluewater valuation_date and valuation_basis columns. Dates for typed values are written in a notes column.
- Evidence coverage and stale share: calculated by Arjun in the consolidation workbook (MANUAL). Private valuations older than 120 days are marked stale (policy).

### 5. Exposure (3 pages)
KPIs: Single-issuer concentration (K09), Private-market allocation (K10), Unhedged FX exposure (K11).
- Single-issuer, private allocation and FX: Wei's workbook (T10), built from the consolidation workbook values plus look-through lines typed from manager reports (MANUAL).
- Policy limits: investment policy.
Pain points: private funds and look-through are not in the weekly view; the Meridian exposure appears only because Wei built it by hand; the look-through limit is not defined in the policy.

### 6. Policy compliance and exceptions (1 page)
KPI: Policy exceptions awaiting approval (K08).
- Breaches against ranges and limits: Wei's workbook.
- Exceptions already approved: Mei's notes and previous minutes, typed.
Pain point: there is no list of approved exceptions and their end dates in a file.

### 7. Manager updates and fee summary (2 pages)
- Manager news: Mei's notes and manager reports in the folder (S04).
- Fee summary appendix: copied forward from the last pack by Sara. One of three copies of the fee list; nobody checks it. It disagrees with Wei's workbook on two holdings.

### 8. Decisions requested and action log (1 page)
Typed by Mei and Sara from the last minutes.

### 9. Appendix: documents needing review (1 page)
KPI: Documents needing review (K14).
Notices and reports with unresolved questions: from the tracker notes column and Sara's memory. Not produced from a system.

### 10. Performance versus benchmark and manager review (quarterly, 1 page; not in the monthly pack today)
KPIs: Excess return versus benchmark (K16), Benchmark coverage of holdings (K17), Manager performance versus benchmark (K15).
- Fund and manager returns: Performance_Workbook.xlsx (S10), built by Arjun by hand from the custody positions file, Bluewater NAV, manager statements (PDF attachments by email) and index levels pasted from the market data vendor and the index provider (MANUAL).
- The page shows two managers ahead, one behind and five funds with no benchmark (see 13). Private figures are a quarter or more behind public ones.
Pain points: benchmarks are chosen in three places; "return" has three meanings; nobody owns the choice; the page is issued about 11 weeks after quarter-end and the same figures go to HR for the bonus round (see 14).

### 11. Compensation inputs (HR only, not in the pack)
KPI: Compensation inputs reconciled (K18). The pack carries none of it; the pay workbook is personal data. HR receives the performance figures from Finance by spreadsheet.

## Review and sign-off

Mei reviews the whole pack one to two times. The pack carries no sign-off page; approval is Mei's email to Sara. The committee's minutes record decisions and are kept by Sara. The pack is a PDF; the workbooks are not circulated, so committee members cannot check a number back to its source.
