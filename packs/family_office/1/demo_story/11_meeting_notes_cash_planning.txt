INTERNAL MEETING NOTES - CASH PLANNING AND CAPITAL CALLS
Illustrative Lakeside Family Office
Date: Tuesday 22 September 2026, 10:00 to 11:00
Present: Mei Tan (chair), Arjun Rao, Sara Lim, Tom Ng, Wei Chen
Notes taken by: Sara Lim
(Fictional meeting. All amounts illustrative.)

1. WHY WE MET

Mei opened: the 17 September committee saw the Harborview IV call as already paid. The committee asked why it was told after the fact, and whether we could have been paying for something we did not know was coming. She wants to be able to say, a month ahead, what we must pay and whether we can afford another commitment.

2. WHAT HAPPENED WITH THE HARBORVIEW IV CALL

Sara went through the timeline.
- Notice letter dated Tuesday 1 September, due Tuesday 15 September (ten business days).
- First copy went to a former contact address. Manager resent on Tuesday 8 September at 16:42.
- Sara read it on Wednesday morning but could not log it until Thursday 10 September, because the pack for the previous cycle was still open.
- She told Tom by chat message the same day.
- Tom checked the USD account: USD 1.0 million, short by USD 0.5 million. He bought USD on Friday 11 September. Settlement is two working days, so the cash arrived on Tuesday 15 September, the due date.
- Arjun released the payment before the 15:00 cut-off. Paid on time, but with no margin.

Tom: "If the FX deal had slipped a day we would have been late and paid interest."
Arjun: "And I only knew when Tom asked me to release it. Nothing told me it was coming."

3. HOW LATE IS LATE

Sara checked the tracker for the last twelve months: 34 notices. Her estimate: median about five working days from receipt to due date, though the agreements say ten. Median two working days from receipt to logging; eleven were logged three or more working days after they arrived.
Mei: "So we lose half the time before we even start, and the other half is ours."
Tom: lead time to fund is two working days for USD, one for the money-market fund. Anything under three working days is tight.
Sara: there is nobody to read the mailbox when she is away. Last month she was out two days and a Kestrel notice sat.
Wei asked whether corrected notices are an extra payment. Sara: no, a replacement, but only if she marks the old row superseded by hand, and she does not always.

4. THE FORWARD VIEW

Mei asked what the funding headroom looks like. Tom's rough view, not in any file: cash and money-market about SGD 27 million; reserve SGD 5 million; known operating costs and distributions about SGD 3.6 million over six months; expected calls over six months about USD 8 million. That leaves headroom of about SGD 7 to 8 million in a base case. Under the stress case (calls multiplied by 1.75, no distributions) it is about break-even, slightly negative.
Mei: "That is the number I want every month, not a hand-built estimate."
Arjun: the pack's funding page only shows calls we have already been told about. Expected calls come from Tom.

5. EXPOSURE TO ONE NAME

Mei asked what our total exposure to Meridian Cold Chain Holdings is across Harborview III, Harborview IV and the Tidewater co-investment. Wei: about SGD 12 million, roughly 5 per cent, but it took him most of a week, and the private funds are not in the weekly report. Mei: "I should be able to ask that on a Tuesday and have the answer on a Tuesday."

6. THE FEE LIST

Arjun noticed that Kestrel's management fee is one and a half per cent in the tracker and one and a quarter per cent in Wei's workbook. Mei asked who owns the fee list. Silence. Sara said it was the analyst's, before the seat became vacant; Wei said he copied his from a presentation. Three copies, no owner, differences on at least three holdings.

7. WHAT WE AGREED

- This is a data and process problem, not a Sara problem. Nobody to be blamed.
- Mei will run a structured workshop with Signal Harbor to find and rank the problems properly. Date: 3 October 2026.
- Sara will send Mei the written call procedure, the tracker (amounts masked) and the last two packs as pre-read.
- Arjun will list the bank and administrator files and who sees them.
- Wei will send the exposure workbook (names masked if needed).
- Tom will write down how he builds the expected-calls and stress view.
- No change to the process before the workshop.
