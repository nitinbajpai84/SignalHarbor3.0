# 13 - Quarterly performance review note (Q2 2026, issued mid-September)

From: Arjun Rao, Finance Lead. To: Mei Tan, Head of Investments. Copy: Lina Ho, HR Lead (for the bonus round). Illustrative Lakeside Family Office (ILFO). All figures are invented, round and net of fees. This is the note as it exists today: built by hand in Performance_Workbook.xlsx, issued 11 weeks after the 30 June quarter-end.

## The question

Are the managers we invested in ahead of their benchmark (positive excess return, "alpha") or behind it? Excess return here is the fund's net return minus the benchmark's return over the same period and currency.

## Result in one table (illustrative)

| Fund | Manager | Benchmark used in this note | Measure | Fund (net) | Benchmark | Excess return | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MGE | Marlowe and Finch Asset Management | MSCI World index (global equity) | 12 months to 30 Jun 2026, time-weighted | 12.0% | 10.5% | +1.5 points | Ahead |
| HPE3 | Harborview Capital Partners | Private-equity benchmark (illustrative) | Net IRR since 2021, to 30 Jun 2026 | 15.0% | 12.0% | +3.0 points | Ahead |
| SGF2 | Seaglass Ventures | Private-equity benchmark (illustrative) | Net IRR since 2022, to 31 Mar 2026 | 6.0% | 11.0% | -5.0 points | Behind |

Two managers are ahead and one is behind.

## What sits behind the table

- **Public equity, US sleeve.** ILFO's directly held US shares are measured against an S&P 500 index. They are not a manager, so they are not in the table. The US sleeve is ahead of that index by about 0.5 points over 12 months (illustrative).
- **Private funds are late.** Harborview III is to 30 June 2026. Seaglass is only to 31 March 2026, because its Q2 statement (a PDF attached to an email) had not arrived. Manager statements come 30 to 60 days after quarter-end. The public figure runs to 30 June. The three are not the same date.
- **Seaglass is young.** A 2022 growth fund is often behind in the early years. The note does not say whether that is a poor manager or an early-stage curve. The committee decides.
- **Not covered.** Harborview IV (too young), Ironbridge Infrastructure I and Logistics Property II, Kestrel Private Credit II and Tidewater Co-Investment 1 have no agreed benchmark or no history. They are not in the table. Five of eight holdings have no verdict.

## Cautions the author wants the reader to see

1. Benchmarks were chosen in three places (Arjun's workbook, Mei's slide, the manager's own report). For MGE the manager's report uses a different index from the one above. Switching to it would turn +1.5 into +0.8 (illustrative).
2. "Return" appears in three versions: time-weighted return for MGE, net IRR for private funds, and a simple change in value in the old pack page. They do not compare with each other.
3. Index levels were pasted from a vendor download on 11 September. Nobody can say which day's close was used for the 30 June level.
4. Nobody has agreed a rule for how many periods behind counts as "lagging".
