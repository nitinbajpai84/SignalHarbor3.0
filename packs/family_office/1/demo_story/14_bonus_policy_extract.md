# 14 - Bonus and manager-review policy (extract)

Illustrative Lakeside Family Office (ILFO). Extract from the Compensation Policy, version 2026.1, owned by the family council, administered by Lina Ho (HR Lead). All amounts and weights are illustrative round figures. Individual pay is not shown anywhere in this folder.

## 1. Who is covered

The investment team: the Head of Investments and the Investment Analyst seat (vacant seven months, so its share is not paid out). The Finance Lead, Risk Analyst and Operations Manager are paid a bonus on a separate scorecard that does not use performance.

## 2. The bonus pool (investment team)

- Target pool for the year: SGD 600 thousand (illustrative), set by the family council.
- Paid once a year, after the Q4 performance note. A quarterly accrual is recorded in the quarterly pay review.

| Component | Weight | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| Excess return of the public portfolio against its benchmarks | 30% | 3-year time-weighted excess return, net of fees (S&P 500 index for the US sleeve, MSCI World index for global equity) |
| Excess return of the private portfolio against its benchmark | 30% | Net IRR of each fund since inception minus the private-equity benchmark, weighted by value |
| Funding and risk discipline | 20% | No breach of the reserve rule or concentration limits without an approved exception |
| Process and judgement | 20% | Head of Investments' review, agreed with the family council |

If the combined excess return is negative, the performance part is capped at half of its weight.

## 3. Manager review

- Each manager is reviewed once a year against its benchmark.
- A manager behind its benchmark for two reviews in a row goes on a watch list. A third review behind leads to a decision on whether to stop adding capital.
- The committee decides. The policy does not say which benchmark applies to which fund, or who may change it.

## 4. Where the numbers come from (and the weakness)

The policy says "performance as reported by Finance". In practice HR receives a spreadsheet from Finance by email some weeks after the performance note. The policy has no rule for:

- which return method counts (time-weighted, net IRR, or change in value);
- who approves the benchmark for each fund;
- what happens when a private fund's final values change after the bonus is paid.

## 5. Personal data and access

The Pay_Review_Workbook.xlsx holds names, salary bands and bonus amounts. It is personal data. Policy: HR Lead and Head of Investments may see it; Finance sees only totals. In practice the workbook is kept on a shared drive that Finance can open (see 15_data_governance_issues.md).

## 6. Review

The extract is reviewed with the family council once a year. Last reviewed 14 months ago.
