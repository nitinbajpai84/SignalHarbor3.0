# ILFO business glossary (working copy)

Illustrative Lakeside Family Office (ILFO). Started by Sara Lim and added to by whoever needed a term explained. Not reviewed as a whole. Not every term is here and some entries are old. Amounts are invented and round.

Entry format: Term (also called) / Meaning / Owner / How often reported / Where the number comes from / Last updated.

---

## Cash cushion (also: "six-month headroom")
- Meaning: how much cash we would still have, in the worst of the next six months, after paying what we know we owe and keeping the reserve aside.
- Owner: Tom Ng, Treasurer
- Reported: monthly, in the committee pack
- Source: Cash_NAV_Consolidation.xlsx, which Arjun builds from the custody daily file
- Last updated: June 2026

## Reported-value backing (also: "evidence coverage")
- Meaning: how much of what we report as assets is backed by a statement or a report we can show someone.
- Owner: Arjun Rao
- Reported: monthly
- Source: the consolidation workbook and the manager reports folder
- Last updated: May 2026

## Pack effort (also: "pack hours")
- Meaning: the hours the team spends getting the committee pack out of the door, including the redo. Taken from the logged hours of the people involved.
- Owner: Sara Lim
- Reported: after each committee meeting, when someone remembers
- Source: the team's hour logs
- Last updated: April 2026

## Known obligations
- Meaning: the calls and other payments we have committed to pay, in SGD. A capital call becomes a known obligation once it has been paid and shows in the bank file.
- Owner: Tom Ng
- Reported: monthly, with the cash cushion
- Source: the tracker, plus the bank statement
- Last updated: January 2026

## Unfunded commitments (also: "what we still owe the funds")
- Meaning: what we promised to the private funds and have not yet paid in. Goes down when we pay a call and up when a distribution can be called back.
- Owner: Sara Lim
- Reported: monthly, taken on the last business day of the month
- Source: Capital_Call_Tracker.xlsx, checked against the administrator's NAV report
- Last updated: March 2026

## Stress cash need (also: "bad case calls")
- Meaning: extra money we might have to pay in a bad case, on top of what we already know we owe, using the usual stress multiplier on the managers' expected calls.
- Owner: Tom Ng
- Reported: quarterly
- Source: managers' indicative call schedules, held in the consolidation workbook
- Last updated: February 2026

## Open conflicts
- Meaning: differences between two of our numbers that nobody has fixed yet. Only the material ones are reported to the committee; material means big enough to matter.
- Owner: Arjun Rao
- Reported: monthly
- Source: Arjun's reconciliation notes
- Last updated: 2023 (not reviewed since)

## Policy exceptions waiting
- Meaning: breaches of the investment policy that the committee has not yet approved as exceptions. Anything waiting longer than is reasonable should be raised with the family council.
- Owner: Mei Tan
- Reported: monthly
- Source: committee minutes
- Last updated: January 2026

## Exposure (single name)
- Meaning: how much of our money rides on one company, counting what we hold directly and what sits inside our funds.
- Owner: Wei Chen, with Mei Tan signing off
- Reported: monthly, when Wei has time to build it
- Source: Exposure_Workbook.xlsx
- Last updated: March 2026

## Private share (also: "private-market allocation")
- Meaning: the share of our assets that sits in the private funds.
- Owner: Mei Tan
- Reported: monthly
- Source: the exposure workbook and the administrator NAV report
- Last updated: 2022 (written before the co-investment vehicle existed)

## Exposure (currency)
- Meaning: how much we would gain or lose if a currency moved against SGD, after our hedges. Mostly a USD matter.
- Owner: Tom Ng
- Reported: quarterly
- Source: custody positions and the exposure workbook
- Last updated: May 2026

## Old valuations (also: "stale values")
- Meaning: the share of our assets valued on a number that is too old. A private fund value is stale when its valuation date is older than our usual limit.
- Owner: Arjun Rao
- Reported: monthly, as at month end
- Source: administrator NAV report, valuation date column
- Last updated: April 2026

## Value bridge
- Meaning: why the total went up or down since last time: money in and out, markets moving, currency moving, and whatever is left over that we cannot explain.
- Owner: Arjun Rao
- Reported: quarterly, as at month end
- Source: the consolidation workbook
- Last updated: March 2026

## Notices to chase (also: "documents to review")
- Meaning: capital call notices, distribution notices and manager statements that have come in and still have something wrong or missing. Count them at the end of each month; a corrected notice goes on the list too.
- Owner: Sara Lim
- Reported: monthly
- Source: the calls mailbox and the manager reports folder
- Last updated: February 2026

## Manager scorecard (also: "ahead or behind")
- Meaning: whether each manager is ahead of or behind the benchmark, using the return method that fits the fund.
- Owner: Mei Tan
- Reported: quarterly
- Source: the tracker, which Arjun updates when the manager statements arrive
- Last updated: September 2026

## Alpha (also: "excess return")
- Meaning: how far a fund's return is above or below its benchmark. Positive means we are ahead. Reported as percentage points.
- Owner: Arjun Rao
- Reported: quarterly, public funds monthly
- Source: the tracker
- Last updated: September 2026

## Benchmark cover
- Meaning: whether our holdings have a benchmark we have agreed. For example, the global equity fund MGE has one and the infrastructure fund does not.
- Owner: nobody yet (it used to be the Investment Analyst)
- Reported: not reported
- Source: the benchmark tab in the performance workbook, Mei's slide, and the manager reports
- Last updated: 2023

## Pay inputs matched
- Meaning: the share of staff whose bonus and review inputs agree with the approved performance figure. Lina checks it before the quarterly pay review.
- Owner: Lina Ho
- Reported: quarterly
- Source: Pay_Review_Workbook.xlsx and the spreadsheet Finance sends
- Last updated: July 2026

## Defined terms
- Meaning: the share of the terms we use in the pack (return, benchmark, NAV date and so on) that have one agreed meaning and a person who owns it.
- Owner: Mei Tan
- Reported: not yet reported
- Source: this glossary
- Last updated: October 2026
