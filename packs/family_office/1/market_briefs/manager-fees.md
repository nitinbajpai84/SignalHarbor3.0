# Manager fees and benchmarks

Dated: 2026-10-02.

Fee pressure on managers is rising and more investors now judge performance net of fees against a named benchmark. A family office that reads manager results before fees, or compares holdings with no benchmark, will see the gap grow as fees come down for others.

Illustrative context for the committee. It is outside material, not a figure from your own records.
