# Private markets: capital-call pace and distributions

Dated: 2026-10-02.

Managers across the region are calling capital faster than they are returning it, so unfunded commitments are being drawn down sooner than plans assumed. Distributions are slower, which pushes the private-market share of the portfolio up and lets valuations go stale between quarterly reports.
