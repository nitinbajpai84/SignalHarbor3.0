# Singapore and regional rates: what they do to funding headroom

Dated: 2026-10-02.

Short-term Singapore dollar rates have eased a little while US dollar rates stay higher, so carrying a standing US dollar line costs more than the Singapore dollar cash it is set against. For a family office that tightens funding headroom first, and widens the cost of any unhedged dollar exposure.
