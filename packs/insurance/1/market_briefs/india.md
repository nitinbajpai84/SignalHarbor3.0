# India market brief

Dated: 2026-10-01 (prepared for V3-03; no figures included, see status note above).

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India (IRDAI) is India's insurance
regulator, the analogue to MAS/BNM/the Hong Kong Insurance Authority for this pack's three
current LBUs. (Source: general industry knowledge, regulator name and role; not present in this
pack's own kpis.json, which does not cover India -- see templates/insurance/1/glossary.yaml
term "IRDAI".)

The Life Insurance Council is an industry body for Indian life insurers that aggregates and
publishes industry-level new-business statistics, the Indian analogue to LIA Singapore / LIAM.
(Source: general industry knowledge, body name and role; not present in this pack's own
kpis.json -- see templates/insurance/1/glossary.yaml term "Life Insurance Council". The research
note's own section 1.5 separately cites a named Indian insurer's (LIC) FY26 commentary as a
secondary source for the VNB-margin concept, not for any India market-sizing figure.)
