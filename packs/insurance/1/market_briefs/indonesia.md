Dated: 2026-10-01 (prepared for V3-03; no figures included, see status note above).

industry in Indonesia, the analogue role MAS/BNM/the Hong Kong Insurance Authority play for this
pack's three current LBUs (Singapore, Malaysia, Hong Kong).
