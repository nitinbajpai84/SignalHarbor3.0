# Malaysia market brief

Dated: 2026-10-01 (prepared for V3-03; no figures included, see status note above).

The Life Insurance Association of Malaysia (LIAM) is the industry body named in this pack's KPI
tree as the market-indicator source for Malaysia's new-business statistics.
(Source: docs/signal-harbor/cmo-proof/kpis.json K01 market_indicators.)

Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM) is Malaysia's central bank and insurance/takaful regulator; it sets
the Overnight Policy Rate (OPR) named in this pack as a market indicator, and Malaysia's Personal
Data Protection Act (PDPA) governs marketing consent for CEP/engagement activity in this market,
alongside Singapore's. (Source: docs/signal-harbor/cmo-proof/kpis.json K01/K09 market_indicators.)
