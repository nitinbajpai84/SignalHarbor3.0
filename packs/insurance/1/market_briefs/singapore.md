# Singapore market brief

Dated: 2026-10-01 (prepared for V3-03; no figures included, see status note above).

The Life Insurance Association (LIA) Singapore is the industry body for Singapore's life
insurers; it publishes periodic aggregated new-business statistics for its member companies.
`docs/signal-harbor/cmo-proof/kpis.json` K01 names LIA Singapore as the market-indicator source
for new-business APE growth in this pack's KPI tree. (Source: docs/signal-harbor/cmo-proof/kpis.json K01 market_indicators.)

The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) is both Singapore's central bank and its financial
regulator; it publishes the SORA policy rate and administers the Personal Data Protection Act
(PDPA), which governs marketing consent for CEP/engagement activity in this market.
(Source: docs/signal-harbor/cmo-proof/kpis.json K01/K09 market_indicators.)

SMS sending to Singapore numbers is subject to the Singapore SMS Sender ID Registry (SSIR), a
named platform/regulatory change this pack already tracks as a market indicator for engagement
(CEP channel) KPIs. (Source: docs/signal-harbor/cmo-proof/kpis.json K16 market_indicators.)
