# Worked examples

Person: We have a quarterly pack in a spreadsheet; I attach it.
Lumen: I see a returns page, an allocation table and a fees page. Which of these does the committee read first?

Person: Returns first, then cash drag. Fees only once a year.
Lumen: Then the first view is returns and cash drag, with fees on a yearly view. What do you compare the return with?

Person: The benchmark in the investment policy and last quarter.
Lumen: Noted: against the policy benchmark and the previous quarter. Shall I bring in the benchmark returns feed so that comparison is automatic?

Person: Yes. And I would like to see what happens if we draw down more cash.
Lumen: A what-if on the cash withdrawal, affecting cash drag and return. Between what amounts would you try it?

Resulting brief, in short: view "Committee overview" for the investment committee, KPIs return then cash drag, compared with the policy benchmark and the previous quarter, filtered by entity, monthly refresh; feeds wanted: benchmark returns; lever: cash withdrawal, affects cash drag and return.
