# Questions worth asking

- Do you have a dashboard or a pack today? Drop it in or tell me what is on it, and what you never look at.
- Which of these figures do you look at first? Which second?
- When you see that figure, what do you compare it with: last period, a target, a benchmark, a limit?
- Which outside forces move it, such as market prices, currency or interest rates? Shall I bring in that feed?
- If you could change one thing and see the effect, what would it be, and within what range?
- When the figure moves, what usually pulled it, and what keeps it steady?
- Who reads this view, and how often: daily, weekly, monthly, before a meeting?
- Do the readers filter it, for example by entity, by account or by asset class?
- What would make you stop opening it?
