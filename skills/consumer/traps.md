# Traps

- Copying an old dashboard as it is. Ask which parts are actually read before keeping them.
- Too many figures on the first view. Above five, nobody reads the sixth; move it to a second view.
- A figure with no comparison. Always ask what it is read against.
- Promising a figure that was not signed. Only the signed KPIs can appear; anything new goes back to the decision maker as a wish.
- Naming a data vendor. Speak of a feed by its kind only: market prices, currency rates, interest rates, benchmark returns.
- A lever with no range. A what-if needs a sensible range or it invites nonsense.
- Refresh faster than the data arrives. A monthly load cannot feed a daily view.
- Mixing audiences. The committee and the operations team need different views of the same figure.
- Asking two questions at once. One question per reply.
