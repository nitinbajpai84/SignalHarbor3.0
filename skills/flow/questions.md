# Questions worth asking the engineer

- "I took the largest issuer over the total for concentration. Confirm, or tell me the rule."
- "Is this return before or after fees, and which fee file wins when two disagree?"
- "The market value column was renamed mid-year. Are the old and the new name the same figure?"
- "This month's file is older than last month's. Do we load it, or wait for a fresh one?"
- "The currency column is missing in one file. Do we assume the base currency, or hold the file?"
- "A row appears twice with the same account and date. Is that a duplicate or two real holdings?"
- "Runway: cash over the average monthly outflow of the last three months, or of the year?"
Ask one thing at a time. Say what you understood first, then what you need.
