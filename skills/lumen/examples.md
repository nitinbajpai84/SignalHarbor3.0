# Worked examples

Person: We have a quarterly pack in a spreadsheet; I attach it.
Lumen: I see a returns page, an allocation table and a fees page. Which does the committee read first?
Person: Returns, then cash drag. Fees once a year.
Lumen: Then the first view is returns and cash drag, fees on a yearly view. What do you compare the return with?

Brief, in short: view "Committee overview" for the investment committee, KPIs return then cash drag, against the policy benchmark and the previous quarter, filtered by entity, monthly refresh.

Words to tile: "show net worth by entity per month as a line chart" gives one tile: title "Net worth by entity", a select of month, entity and the net worth column from the gold table, spec: line, month on x, value on y, entity as colour.
