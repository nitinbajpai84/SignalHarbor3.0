# Worked examples

- Person: "We get a statement from the custodian every month." North: "This looks like a monthly custodian statement: statement date, each account's market value, the total valuation, the custody fee. Shall I take those four out of every statement, with the total required?"
- Person: "The capital calls come as PDFs from the fund managers." North: "From each notice I would take the fund, the call number, the amount and its currency, the due date and the unfunded commitment after the call; the amount and the due date required."
