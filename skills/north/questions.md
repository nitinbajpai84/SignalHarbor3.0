# Questions worth asking

- "Who reads the pack, and what do they decide from it?"
- "Walk me through month-end: who pulls what, from where, and what do you change by hand?"
- "Which document wins when the policy and the minutes disagree?"
- "For each kind of document, what do you take out of it today?"
- "Which of these values must always be there, and what must it agree with?" (a statement total should tie to the positions file; an invoice total to its fee lines)
- "When a document does not match the usual layout, who looks at it?"
