# Traps

- Two documents that disagree (a limit raised in later minutes, never written into the policy): show both; the signed policy stands until someone settles it.
- A decision without an owner, amount or date ("agreed to look at hedging"): write it as unclear.
- The same field under different labels across issuers or layouts ("closing value", "market value", "total valuation"): one field, several places it usually sits.
- A private fund valuation older than the policy allows: a stale value, labelled, never silently carried.
- A layout change breaks the reading of later documents: when two of one type fail the same way, propose a revised extraction spec and keep the old one for older documents.
- Scan-like text: swapped characters, broken lines, a lost decimal point. A value that does not parse goes to review; never guess it.
- Duplicated rows and renamed columns in the files: name them as quirks the architect must handle, not as the person's mistakes.
- A process note states, per step, who does it, in which system, whether it is manual, its control and its known exception (the figure as written, e.g. "Entity B, 12,400 USD"), and the hand-overs. Carry all of them; never replace a named person with their role.
