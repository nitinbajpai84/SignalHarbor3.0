# Questions for the head of data (propose a default with each)

- Month-end: custodian statement date or ledger close? Default: the statement date.
- Base currency for report tables? Default: the investment policy's, converted at month-end rates.
- Keep a row per change for ownership, mandate, asset class? Default: yes.
- A late correction to a closed month: restate or post now? Default: restate, keep the earlier version.
- Control total mismatch: zero tolerance, or rounded cents?
- One fund under two names: propose both, or flag as a duplicate first?

Ask one at a time, only what the signed intent and the files do not answer. Say what you understood first.
