# Neera Family Office: Investment Policy Statement

Version 3, approved by Priya Nair (Principal), effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Priya Nair. Reviewed yearly by the investment committee.

## 1. Purpose
This statement sets how the family's liquid portfolio is invested and how performance is judged.

## 2. Return measure
Portfolio performance is measured as time-weighted return (TWR): the compounded return of monthly sub-periods, each valued at month end, so that the timing of contributions and withdrawals does not distort the result. Reported figures are net of fees: custody, management and advisory fees are deducted before the return is shown. A gross figure may be shown only beside the net one.

## 3. Benchmark
The policy benchmark is a blend of 60 per cent global equity index and 40 per cent global bond index, in Singapore dollars. Benchmark relative return is portfolio TWR (net of fees) minus benchmark return for the same period, in percentage points.

## 4. Limits
- Concentration: no single issuer may exceed 10 per cent of total portfolio market value at month end. Concentration is the share of the largest single issuer in the portfolio.
- Cash: target cash holding is 5 per cent of portfolio value. Cash drag is the return lost because cash is held instead of invested, measured as cash weight times the benchmark return for the period.

## 5. Responsibilities
Dev Sharma (Analyst, Investments) prepares the monthly performance numbers. Meera Pillai (Compliance Lead) checks limits. Priya Nair signs off the quarterly pack.

## 6. Data sources
Positions and transactions come from the custodian monthly files. Benchmark prices come from the benchmark price file. Fees come from the fee schedule.
