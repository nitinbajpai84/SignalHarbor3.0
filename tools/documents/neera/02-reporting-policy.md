# Reporting Policy

Owner: Sameer Khan (Reporting Officer). Approved by Arjun Mehta (Head of Operations), 15 February 2026.

## Cadence
A monthly report is issued to the Principal by working day 8. A quarterly pack goes to the family by working day 12 after quarter end.

## Figures every report must show
1. Time-weighted return, net of fees, for the month, quarter and year to date.
2. Benchmark relative return for the same periods.
3. Cash drag, in percentage points.
4. Fee ratio: total fees paid in the period divided by average portfolio value, shown as a percentage of assets, annualised.
5. Concentration: the largest single issuer as a percentage of the portfolio.

## Rules
- A figure may be published only for a period that Operations has confirmed as loaded.
- Every figure must be traceable to a source file and a load run.
- Corrections after publication are shown as a restated figure with a note, never silently overwritten.
- Reports are prepared from the reporting database, not from spreadsheets kept by hand. Sameer Khan keeps the hand-built pack only until the database is accepted.
