# Fee agreement summary

Between Neera Family Office and its service providers, effective 1 April 2026. Owner: Arjun Mehta.

- Custody (Northgate Custody): 0.80 per cent a year of average assets, billed monthly.
- Management (the appointed fund managers): 0.45 per cent a year of assets under management, taken inside fund prices.
- Advisory (investment adviser): 0.20 per cent a year, billed quarterly in arrears.

"Net of fees" in all reports means after these three fees. The fee schedule file records the rates; the accounting system records what was paid.
