# Data access policy

Owner: Meera Pillai (Compliance Lead). Approved by Priya Nair, 1 March 2026.

- The Principal and the Head of Operations may see all figures and all source files.
- Investments staff may see positions, transactions and performance, but not the general ledger or personal client contact details.
- Reporting and Treasury staff may see cash, fees and reports; they may not edit source files.
- Client personal details (names, addresses, tax numbers) are restricted to Compliance and the Principal.
- Any change to who can see what needs written approval from Meera Pillai.
- Access is reviewed every six months.
