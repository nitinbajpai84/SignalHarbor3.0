# Cash management guideline

Owner: Rohan Das (Treasury Officer). 5 May 2026.

Cash is held in Pier Bank current and money market accounts and in the custodian cash line. Daily balances are exported from the bank portal. Cash weight is cash divided by total portfolio value at month end. Cash above the 5 per cent target for two months running is raised with Priya Nair. Cash drag, as used in reports, is cash weight times benchmark return for the period.
