# Family reporting FAQ

Owner: Sameer Khan.

**What is time-weighted return?** The growth of the portfolio with the effect of money paid in or out removed.
**Why net of fees?** It is what the family actually keeps.
**What is the concentration figure?** The largest single issuer as a share of the portfolio; the limit is in the investment policy.
**Why is the fee ratio late?** It needs the accounting trial balance, which is posted around working day 6.
